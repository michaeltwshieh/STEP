## OPERATIVE PROVISIONS

## 1. Appointment

The Appointor, in exercise of the power of appointment reserved by clause [x] of the Trust, hereby irrevocably appoints and declares that the Appointed Fund shall henceforth be held by the Trustee upon trust for the Beneficiary absolutely, freed and discharged from the terms of the Settlement.

## 2. Trustee's acknowledgment

The Trustee hereby acknowledges that it will comply with such appointment and confirms that the trusts, powers and provisions contained in the Trust shall continue to apply to the remaining assets and property of the Trust.

## 3. Payment of tax and expenses

Any tax, costs and expenses and other liabilities occasioned by the appointment shall be borne by the Beneficiary.

## 4. Trustee's lien

Nothing in this Deed shall prejudice or impair in any way any lien or charge to which the Trustee is entitled in respect of any liabilities for which it may be or become accountable.

## 5. Release and Indemnity of Trustee

## 5.1 Release

The Beneficiary, so far as he [she] is competent to do so, releases the Trustee entirely from all claims, demands, actions, proceedings and accounts of any kind on the part of any person (whether in existence or not) actually or prospectively interested under the Trust or any act or thing done or omitted in execution or purported execution of such trusts other than and except only actions arising from any fraud, wilful misconduct or gross negligence in which the Trustee was a party.

## 5.2 Indemnity of Trustee

The Beneficiary hereby covenants with the Trustee to keep it fully and effectually indemnified entirely from and against all existing, future or contingent liabilities, claims, demands, actions, proceedings and accounts of any kind (and any costs and expenses incurred in connection therewith) which may be brought or made or threatened to be brought or made by any person against the Trustee in connection with the Trust or any instruments or deeds supplemental thereto including this Deed or in any way relating to the Trust Fund or any act or thing done by the Trustee arising directly or indirectly or omitted in execution or purported execution of the trusts of the Trust and such indemnity shall, without prejudice to the generality of the foregoing, extend to any claims or demands for taxes, duties or other fiscal liabilities payable in any part of the world on or in respect of the Trust Fund arising or made at any time and whether or not such claims or demands are legally enforceable against the Old Trustee in its jurisdiction of residence PROVIDED ALWAYS THAT:

- (1) such indemnity shall not operate to indemnify the Trustee for liabilities it is not entitled to an indemnity for under the terms of the Trust;
- (2) the liability of the Beneficiary under this indemnity shall be limited to the value of the Appointed Fund.
