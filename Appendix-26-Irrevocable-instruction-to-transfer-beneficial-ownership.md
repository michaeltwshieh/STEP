# Appendix 26: Irrevocable instruction to transfer beneficial ownership

## Appendix 26:

## Irrevocable instruction to transfer beneficial ownership

## IRREVOCABLE INSTRUCTION TO TRANSFER BENEFICIAL OWNERSHIP

To:

[Nominee shareholder]

From:

[Beneficial owner]

Re:

[Company under management] (the 'Company')

Please be advised that I, [beneficial owner name and address] have agreed to transfer to [transferee, name and address] my beneficial ownership of the shares in the Company, which shares you currently hold as nominee on my behalf.

You are hereby irrevocably instructed to cancel the existing Shareholder Nominee Declaration. By endorsing this memorandum with his consent, [name of transferee] instructs you to execute a new Shareholder Nominee Declaration in his favour and make all consequential amendments to the corporate documentation, including, but not limited to, the register of beneficial ownership, and FATCA and CRS disclosure forms.

DATE

SIGNED

ENDORSEMENT

DATE

SIGNED
