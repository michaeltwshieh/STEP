# Appendix 10A: Resolution of board to migrate company by way of continuance process

## Appendix 10A:

## Resolution of board to migrate company by way of continuance process

## AB Limited (Company [number])

## WRITTEN RESOLUTONS OF THE SOLE DIRECTOR OF AB LIMITED (the 'Company')

Pursuant to art.[x] of the Company's Articles of Association, the undersigned sole director of the Company for the time being, HEREBY:

1. NOTES THAT the Company is a private company limited by shares, having been incorporated in [jurisdiction] on [date] pursuant to [companies legislation] and has been allocated company number [number].
2. RESOLVES that it is desirable and in the interests of the Company to (a) de-register and migrate from the register of companies pursuant to Part [x] of the [companies legislation] of the laws of [old jurisdiction] and (b) re-domicile in [new jurisdiction] by way of continuation process pursuant to Part [x] of [companies legislation] the laws of [new jurisdiction].
3. [RESOLVES that it is appropriate in the circumstances to comply with the laws of [new jurisdiction] to adopt new Articles in the form attached and marked 'AB1' .]
4. RESOLVES that the written resolution procedure pursuant to art.[x] be used to pass, if the members think fit, the special resolution required to authorise the migration described in resolution 2 above, as set out in the written resolution attached marked 'AB2' .
5. RESOLVES that, subject to the passing of the special resolution referred to above, the secretary/registered agent/corporate administrator be and is hereby authorised to complete all necessary and desirable actions and procedures to enable the Company to migrate as aforesaid.

DATE

SIGNED  \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ ([For and on behalf of] Sole Director]
