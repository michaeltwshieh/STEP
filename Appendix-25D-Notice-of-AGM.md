# Appendix 25D: Notice of AGM

## Appendix 25D: Notice of AGM

## AB LIMITED (Company [number]) NOTICE OF ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING

To:

The members of the Company

The directors of the Company

[The auditors of the Company]

NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN THAT the Annual General Meeting of the Company will take place at [venue] on [date] at [time] for the following purposes:

1. To consider and, if thought fit, approving the financial statements of the Company for the year ending [date].
2. To receive the report of the directors and consider their recommendation for a declaration of a dividend.
3. To re-appoint the following director [who retires by rotation].
4. To re-appoint the auditors and authorise the directors to fix their remuneration.

DATE

SIGNED

Note: A member of the Company who is entitled to attend and vote is also entitled to appoint a proxy, who need not be a member of the Company, to attend, speak and vote instead of him.
