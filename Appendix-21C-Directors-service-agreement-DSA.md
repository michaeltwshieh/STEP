# Appendix 21C: Director's service agreement (DSA)

## Appendix 21C:

## Director's service agreement (DSA)

## THIS DIRECTOR SERVICES AGREEMENT

## BETWEEN

- (1) [Name of new company] , having its registered office at [address] (the 'Company'), and
- (2) [Name of director], its registered office located at [address] (the 'Director')

## WHEREAS

- A. [The Service Provider] introduced the Director to the Company.
- B. The Company wishes to appoint the Director as sole director of the Company.
- C. The business of the Company is that of an asset-holding company.

## NOW IT IS AGREED THAT :

The terms and conditions upon which the Director is engaged as sole director of the Company are as follows:

## 1. Definitions

- 1.1 In this Agreement, the following words shall bear the following meanings, save where otherwise provided or where the context otherwise requires or admits:
- 1.1.1 'Articles' means the memorandum and articles of association of the Company for the time being in force.
- 1.1.2 'Board' means the board of directors for the time being of the Company.
- 1.1.3 ['Company Documents' means any of the following documents to which the Company is a party or which otherwise relate to the Company: limited partnership agreement; investment management agreement; investment advisory agreement; directors' services agreement; offering document or other agreement with other service providers including (without limitation) the administrator and and the custodian; and Articles of Association]. [ This will vary by entity type .]
- 1.2 Unless the context otherwise requires, words importing the singular number shall include the plural, words importing any gender shall include all genders, and words importing persons only shall include companies and associations or bodies of persons, whether incorporated or not, and vice versa.

## 2. Appointment and Term

- 2.1 The Company agrees to appoint the Director as sole director of the Company, and the Director agrees to act as sole director of the Company in accordance with the provisions of this Agreement.
- 2.2 This Agreement shall have effect from the date hereof and shall remain in force and effect until terminated in accordance with its terms.

## 3. Remuneration

- 3.1 The Company shall pay the following fees to the Director (the 'Directorship Fees'):
- 3.1.1 An Annual Fee specified in Schedule 1 for each year or part year that the Director serves as sole director of the Company.  The Annual Fee is for the provision of services to the Company in respect of certain statutory functions and one annual in person meeting, payable annually in advance.
- 3.1.2 Additional fees will be charged on a time spent basis for all matters which require effort and time on the part of the Director at the Hourly Rate specified in Schedule 1 and billed at a frequency to be determined by the Director.
- 3.2 The Director may vary the fees and provisions for expenses payable to it under this Agreement, provided that notice shall be given to the Company prior to the effective date of such variation.

## 4. Expenses

- 4.1 The Company shall, upon being provided with reasonable proof of expenditure, reimburse all reasonable expenses incurred by the Director in the course of carrying out his or its duties under this Agreement, including but not limited to:
- 4.1.1 all reasonable travelling and other expenses incurred by the Director in attending meetings of the Board or equivalent or any committee of the Board, or general or extraordinary meetings of the Company; and
- 4.1.2 telephone, fax, printing, copying charges, which shall be charged at [number]% of the value of invoices for the Annual Fee and/or time costs; and
- 4.1.3 courier charges (together, the 'Expenses').

## 5. Time and attention

- 5.1 The Director shall accept and hold office as sole director of the Company subject to and in accordance with this Agreement, the Articles, applicable law and subject to and with the benefit of the indemnification and other provisions contained in the Articles and this Agreement.
- 5.2 The Director shall discharge his or its duties and obligations as director of the Company in accordance with the Company Documents and laws of [jurisdiction].
- 5.3 Company shall ensure that appropriate service providers as reasonably determined by the Director, shall be engaged, to assist the Director with his or its duties. During the continuance of this Agreement the Director shall not be

required to devote his or its whole time and attention to the business of the Company and the Director may provide services of a like nature to any other person, firm or company.

## 6. Delegation

- 6.1 Subject to the Articles, the Director may delegate the whole or any part of his or its functions, powers, discretions, duties and obligations hereunder to any person, firm or corporation, and any such delegation may be on such terms and conditions as the Director thinks fit.  The Director shall not incur any liability whatsoever arising from the negligence, wilful default or fraud of any delegate or agent so appointed or employed.

## 7. Confidentiality

- 7.1. The Director shall not (except in the proper course of the Director's duties) during or after the period of his or its engagement under this Agreement divulge to any person whatever or otherwise make use of (and shall use best endeavours to prevent the publication or disclosure of) any trade secret or any confidential information concerning the business or finances of the Company.
- 7.2. Nothing in this clause 7 shall prevent the Director from making filings and disclosures as required by the laws of [jurisdiction] or any other applicable law.

## 8. Termination

- 8.1 In the event of termination by the Company, which must be upon three months' notice, the Director will resign as sole director as of the expiration of the notice period or such other earlier date as may be agreed, provided that the Directorship Fees due have been paid.
- 8.2 In the event of termination by the Director, which must be upon three months' notice, the Director shall continue to provide services under this Agreement until the expiry of the notice period or until such other date as may be agreed.
- 8.3 This Agreement may be terminated forthwith by and the Director on giving notice in writing to the Company if at any time the Company shall go into liquidation or receivership or shall have an examiner appointed (except for a voluntary liquidation for the purposes of reconstruction or amalgamation upon terms previously approved in writing by the notifying party) or the party notified shall be unable to pay its debts as they fall due;
- 8.4 The termination of this Agreement shall be without prejudice to any rights that may have accrued hereunder to any party hereto prior to such termination, including any right of indemnity and accrued but unpaid fees or expenses.

## 10. Representations and Warranties

- 10.1. The Company represents and warrants to the Directors and FFP that during the term of the Director's appointment:
- 10.1.1 the Company is a company in good standing in any jurisdiction in which it carries on business and is fully empowered to enter into and conform to the terms and conditions contained herein;
- 10.1.2 the Company is not involved in any civil, criminal, or administrative actions or proceedings and has not been involved in any such actions or proceedings for five years prior to the date hereof, as applicable;
- 10.1.3 all final and executed versions of the Company Documents and any other material documents, including but not limited to, marketing materials, performance tables, regulatory filings and licences, and administration, custody, prime brokerage or other service provider agreements (each as may be revised or amended from time to time and collectively referred to herein as the "Material Documents"), provided to the Director by the Company, its sponsors or professional advisers, are true, complete and correct copies of the Material Documents to the best extent of the Company's knowledge;
- 10.1.4 it has supplied to the Director all relevant information which might reasonably be expected to materially affect the decision of the Director to act as such and the Company shall immediately make available to the Director any further relevant material information that may either become available or be reasonably requested hereafter by the Director.
- 10.2 The Company acknowledges that no provision of this Agreement shall require the Director to:
- 10.2.1 do anything which may be illegal or contrary to any applicable law or regulation;
- 10.2.2.  expend or risk their own funds or otherwise incur any expense or other financial liability in the performance of any of their duties and obligations.
- 10.2.3 the Director may, at any time, do or refrain from doing, any act if, in his or its absolute discretion, considers it proper to do so in accordance with the duties to the Company and any applicable law.
- 10.2.4 The Director represents and warrants to the Company that during the term of the Directors' appointment, in acting as sole director of the Company, the Director will comply with the statutory, regulatory and common law duties and obligations applicable under the laws of [jurisdiction]

## 11. Notices

- 11.1 Any notice to be delivered under this Agreement shall be in writing and may be delivered by courier service, personal delivery, facsimile or electronic mail to the following addresses: [insert addresses].
- 11.2 Notices shall be deemed to be delivered, when sent by courier or personal delivery, when delivered and when sent by fax or e-mail, on the date stated in the transmission report or email provided that a notice given in accordance with the above but received on a non-working day or after business hours in the place of receipt shall only be deemed to be given on the next working day in that place.

## 12. Insurance

- 12.1 The Company shall obtain and maintain adequate insurance for the Director, in terms and for amounts reasonably satisfactory to the Director, in respect of any liability the Director may incur as a result of acting as directors of the Company (the 'Insurance').  Notwithstanding the foregoing, the Company will not be required to obtain the Insurance where the Directors, in their discretion, determine the cost associated with obtaining and maintaining the Insurance is prohibitive, and as such not in the best interests of the Company.

## 13. Miscellaneous

- 13.1 In the event that the Director, in furtherance of the duties as director of the Company, require professional advice with respect to the affairs of the Company, the Director may consult the Company's legal, tax and other professional advisers at the Company's expense.
- 13.2 The obligations under this Agreement shall not be assignable by the parties.
- 13.3 No amendment, waiver or variation of this Agreement shall be effective unless it is made in writing and executed by each of the parties hereto.
- 13.4 This Agreement is governed by and shall be construed in accordance with the laws of [jurisdiction] and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of the said jurisdiction.
