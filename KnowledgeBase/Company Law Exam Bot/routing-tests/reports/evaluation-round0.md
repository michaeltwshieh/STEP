## KAP differences and source conflicts

- KAP 1.1 contains winding-up, liquidator appointment and no-audit resolutions. Both
  candidates omit audit; B also omits appointment.
- KAP 2.1 says a sole director cannot hold a board meeting. Exam arts 20.4-20.5 deem a
  sole director a quorum and permit signed action. The supported outcome remains a
  written sole-director resolution.
- KAP 2.2 adds a floating charge over other assets, while the question supplies only a
  legal mortgage over the aircraft. The question controls; both candidates correctly
  avoid importing the floating-charge fact.
- KAP 3.3 suggests underlying-company directors owe similar duties to beneficiaries.
  Module 8 §2.1 states duties are owed to Company, and frozen S03 records a trust-law
  gap. Both candidates correctly preserve Company as duty beneficiary.
- KAP 3.4's change-of-trustee scenario is an illustration, not a legal prerequisite.
- KAP 5.2's shareholder promissory-note loan is ordinary debt under Module 6 §3.
