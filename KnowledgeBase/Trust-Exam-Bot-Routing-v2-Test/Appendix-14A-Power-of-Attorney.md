# Appendix 14A: Power of Attorney

## Appendix 14A: Power of Attorney

## DATE

BY :

[Name of Beneficiary] ('the Beneficiary ')

## RECITALS

The Beneficiary is a member of the class of beneficiaries of the [Name] Trust, a trust established by Deed of Settlement between [Name] ('the Settlor ') and [Name of trust company] ('the Trustee ') on [date] ('the Trust ')

## OPERATIVE PROVISIONS

1. The Beneficiary hereby appoints [Name] ('the Attorney' ) to be his [her] attorney.
2. The Attorney is authorised during the period of [x] months from the date hereof, to receive distributions on behalf of the Beneficiary from the Trustee of the Trust, and to give a valid and lawful receipt therefor.

IN WITNESS the Beneficiary has duly executed this Power of Attorney as a deed on the date above.
