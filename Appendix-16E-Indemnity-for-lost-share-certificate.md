## Appendix 16E: Indemnity for lost share certificate

## INDEMNITY FOR LOST SHARE CERTIFICATE

To: AB Limited (the 'Company')

The original certificate of title relating to the securities set out below issued by the abovenamed Company has been lost or destroyed.

Neither the securities nor the certificate of title have been transferred, charged, lent or deposited or dealt with in any manner affecting the title thereto and the person named in the certificate is the person entitled to be on the register of members of the Company in respect to such securities.

I request the Company to issue a duplicate certificate of title for such securities and in consideration for doing so, undertake to indemnify the Company against all claims and demands which may be made against the Company and/or its directors and officers in consequence of compliance with this request and of the Company allowing at any time hereafter a transfer of the said securities, or any part thereof, without the production of the original certificate.

I undertake to deliver to the Company for cancellation the original certificate should it ever be recovered.
