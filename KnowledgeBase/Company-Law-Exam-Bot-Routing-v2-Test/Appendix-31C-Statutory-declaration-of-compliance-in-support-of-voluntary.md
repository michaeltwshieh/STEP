## Appendix 31C:

## Statutory declaration of compliance in support of voluntary striking off

## AB LIMITED (Company No. [number])

STATUTORY DECLARATION OF COMPLIANCE IN SUPPORT OF APPLICATION FOR STRIKE OFF I, [Name and address of director] do SOLEMNLY AND SINCERELY DECLARE THAT:

1. I am the sole director of AB Limited whose registered office is situated at [address] (the 'Company').
2. The Company has not conducted any business since [date], and is unlikely to recommence operations in the future.
3. The Company has no assets, in particular no real estate located in [jurisdiction of incorporation].
4. The Company has no debts and no outstanding liabilities.
5. There are no claimants with any outstanding claim against the Company and it is not involved in any legal proceedings in any jurisdiction, including insolvency matters.
6. In these circumstances [I am advised and believe that] the Company is eligible to apply for its name to be struck off the register.

Declared at [place] ) by [name] ) this [date], before me, )
