## Appendix 28: Investment management agreement, extracts

## DISCRETIONARY INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT AGREEMENT

## DATED

## BETWEEN

[Name and address of managed company] (the 'Company'), and [Name and address of beneficial owner] (the 'Investment Manager')

## RECITAL

The directors of the Company have asked the Investment Manager to provide discretionary investment and other services to the Company and the Investment Manager has agreed to do so on the terms of this Agreement.

## OPERATIVE PROVISIONS

## IT IS AGREED as follows:

1. Definitions
2. 1.1 'Directors' means the board of directors, or sole director, of the Company
3. 1.2 'Portfolio' means the portfolio of securities and other investments, including cash owned by the Company which are entrusted to the management of the Investment Manager under the terms of this Agreement.
4. 1.3 'Broker' means the entity appointed as broker and custodian of the Portfolio.

## 2. Commencement of Mandate

The terms and provisions of this Agreement shall take effect at the date of its execution and there shall be no time limit within which the assets of the Company must be fully invested.

## 3. Discretionary powers of investment

- 3.1 Subject to the provisions of para.4.1, the Investment Manager may, without reference to or approval from the Directors, invest the assets of the Company in or upon any investments of whatever nature, may buy, sell, retain, deal or trade in any securities wherever situated and in any markets, take day-to-day decisions affecting the Portfolio and otherwise act as the Investment Manager thinks fit and considers appropriate.

- 3.2 The Investment Manager may settle or close-out any transactions without reference to or approval from the Directors, and may debit the Portfolio with any sums required to pay or supplement any deposit or margin in support of such transactions and deposit such security as may be required as collateral.

## 4. Investment objectives [restrictions and limitations]

[Subject to the limitations as determined by the Directors from time to time]

There shall be no restrictions upon:

- 4.1 the type of securities or investments that may be acquired;
- 4.2 the amount of any one or type of security or investment that may be acquired;
- 4.3 the maximum or minimum proportion of the Portfolio as a whole that any one type of security or investment may constitute, or
- 4.4 the markets or exchanges on which transactions may be executed.

## 5. Provision of additional services

The Investment Manager shall, in addition to providing investment management in accordance with para 3 hereof, also provide the following services:

- 5.1 act as agent of the Company in its relations with the Broker and other service providers;
- 5.2 analysis of the performance of the investments and monitoring of risk;
- 5.3 provision of periodic statements to the Company relating to the Portfolio at intervals agreed with the Directors, which statements shall contain details of the composition and value of the Portfolio and measures of performance agreed with the Directors;
- 5.4 maintain such books and records as shall be necessary to record all transactions carried out for the account of the Company.
