## Appendix 4:

## Memorandum: Specimen Summary of Trust

To

The Proposed Settlor [and his [her] Attorney-at-Law]

From

[Name] Trust Company Ltd.

Date

[date]

Re

Your Proposed Discretionary Trust

Following our recent discussions, we attach a first draft of a discretionary trust (the 'Trust') for your consideration and approval and draw your attention to the following main points. Please let us know if you have any comments or questions. We would be most grateful if you would sign a copy of this memorandum as confirmation of its receipt and that you understand its contents and are happy to proceed.

## 1. Settlor and Trustees

You will see that you are described as the Settlor and [Name] Trust Company Ltd. are described as the Trustees.

## 2. The Trust Fund

In accordance with clause 1.1(a), the Trust Fund will initially be an initial nominal sum of US$1.00. The main assets that will comprise the Trust Fund can be transferred by way of additions in accordance with clause 1.1(b). It is essential that the legal title (ownership) of these assets be transferred by employing the correct method of transfer applicable to such assets, such as deed of conveyance, wire transfer, stock transfer form, irrevocable instruction, assignment, as the case may be.

## 3. Beneficiaries

You will see from clause 1.3 that the class of Beneficiaries of the Trust are: (a) your children and remoter issue and (b) the spouses, widows and widowers (but not such widows or widowers who have remarried) of your children and remoter issue.

Pursuant to the powers in clause 2.1, you may add additional persons to the class of Beneficiaries. There is also a power conferred by clause 3.1 to exclude persons from the class.

## 4. Discretionary Trusts

- 4.1 Clause 6.1 confers upon the Trustees a discretionary power to pay or apply income generated by the Trust Fund to or for the benefit of any one or more members of the class of Beneficiaries if and when they think fit.
- 4.2 Clause 6.2.1 confers upon the Trustees a discretionary power to pay or apply (appoint) all or part of the capital of the Trust Fund to any one or more members of the class of the Beneficiaries. The Trustees may make outright distributions to the selected Beneficiaries or alternatively may give them a more limited interest such as a right under new terms.

- 4.3 Clause 6.2.2 confers upon the Trustees a power to pay all or part of the capital of the Trust Fund to the trustees of another trust for the benefit of a selected Beneficiary if this is desirable.
- 4.4 In the unlikely event that any property remains in the Trust Fund at the end of the Trust Period (as defined in clause 1.2), it is to be paid to the [charity].

## 5. Administrative Provisions

Clause 7 includes by reference the administrative provisions contained in the Schedule. The Schedule contains a broad range of fairly standard administrative powers that enable the Trustees to invest and manage the Trust Fund in the best interests of the Beneficiaries.

## 6. Exoneration

Clause 8.2 contains a general exoneration clause that modifies the Trustees' duty of care. It exonerates the Trustees from liability for negligent mistakes that may cause loss. Further, more specific, exoneration clauses are found in clauses 3.1.3, 3.2.2, 7.1, 7.3.2 and 8.2 of the Schedule.

## 7. Trustees' Remuneration

Clause 9 includes a provision that authorises the Trustees to charge for their work. We have attached a document containing our standard published terms and conditions upon which we accept the appointment as Trustee.

## 8. Removal and replacement of trustees

Clause 10 gives you the power exercisable in your lifetime to remove and replace the trustees. [After your death the power is exercisable by the Protector.]

## 9. Flee clause

Clauses 11 through 13 contain flight provisions in case the place of administration of the Trust becomes unsuitable.

## 10. The Trust is Irrevocable

Clause 18 confirms that the Trust is irrevocable, which means that you cannot change your mind at a later date and recover the Trust Fund for your own benefit.

## 11. The exercise of the Trustees' power to distribute and invest the Trust Fund

The Trustees are given very flexible powers to distribute and invest the Trust Fund pending its distribution. We will consider at periodic and regular intervals the manner in which we should exercise these powers. A statement of wishes addressed to us setting out your current suggestions as to the exercise of these powers will give us useful guidance. Your wishes are not binding but may be taken into account by the Trustees when making decisions. You can change your wishes at any time by giving us an amended or further statement.

## 12. Anti-money laundering (AML) and other regulations

## 12.1 AML

You will have received a separate note concerning our anti-money laundering requirements.

## 12.2 FATCA and CRS

You will have received a separate note concerning our disclosure requirements to comply with FATCA and CRS.

## 12.3 Tax

The creation of this Trust and its administration over the Trust Period may have tax consequences for you as Settlor and members of the class of the Beneficiaries. You are advised to obtain independent tax advice from qualified professionals located in the jurisdiction(s) where you and members of the class of Beneficiaries are deemed to reside for tax purposes.
