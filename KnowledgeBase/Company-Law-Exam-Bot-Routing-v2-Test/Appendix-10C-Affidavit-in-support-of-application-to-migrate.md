# Appendix 10C: Affidavit in support of application to migrate

## Appendix 10C: Affidavit in support of application to migrate

IN THE MATTER of an application by AB Limited to migrate by way of continuation process from [jurisdiction] and become re-domiciled in [new jurisdiction pursuant to section [number, companies legislation].

## AFFIDAVIT

I, [name] of [address] MAKE OATH AND SAY [DO SOLEMNLY DECLARE] as follows:

1. I am the sole director of AB Limited (the 'Company').
2. This Affidavit is made in support of an application by the Company to migrate by way of continuation process from [jurisdiction] and become re-domiciled in [new jurisdiction pursuant to section [number, companies legislation].
3. The facts and matters set out in this Affidavit are within my own knowledge, save where the contrary appears and are true. Where the facts and matters are not within my own personal knowledge, they are true to the best of my information and belief and derive from the sources stated.
4. Exhibited to this Affidavit are a bundle of documents marked 'AB1' and reference in this Affidavit to page numbers are to page numbers in Exhibit AB1.

## Corporate documentation

5. There is produced to me in Exhibit 'AB1' true copies of the Company's:
2. 5.1 certificate of incorporation issued by the relevant authority, namely the registrar of companies in [jurisdiction] (page x);
3. 5.2 memorandum of association (pages x) and articles of association (pages x) filed with the registrar of companies of [jurisdiction];
4. 5.3 register of directors and officers, which are required by the laws of [jurisdiction] to be held at its registered office; and
5. 5.4 certificate of good standing issued by the registrar of [jurisdiction], confirming the Company's good standing.

## Company's solvency

6. The Company is solvent. The value of its assets exceed its liabilities and it is in a financial position to pay its debts as they fall due. There is produced to me true copies of the Company's financial statements for the last three years (pages x). Furthermore:
2. 6.1 no petition or other similar proceeding has been filed and remains outstanding or order made or resolution adopted to wind up or liquidate the Company in any jurisdiction;
3. 6.2 no receiver, trustee, administrator or other similar person has been appointed in any jurisdiction and is acting in respect of the Company, its affairs or its property or any part thereof;
4. 6.3 no scheme, order, compromise or other similar arrangement has been entered into or made in any jurisdiction whereby the rights of creditors of the Company are and continue to be suspended or restricted;
5. 6.4 the application for registration is bona fide and not intended to defraud existing creditors of the Company;
6. 6.5 any consent or approval to the transfer required by any contract or undertaking entered into or given by the Company has been obtained, released or waived, as the case may be;
7. I am advised by the Company's attorney-at-law in [jurisdiction], namely [name and address] that:
8. 7.1 the migration contemplated by this application is permitted by and has been approved in accordance with the constitutive documents of the Company; and
9. 7.2 the laws of [jurisdiction] with respect to the migration have been or will be complied with; and
10. 7.3 the Company will, upon registration hereunder, cease to be incorporated, registered or exist under the laws of [jurisdiction].
8. I am advised by the Company's attorney-at-law in [jurisdiction], namely [name and address] that the Company is constituted in a form or substantially a form which could have been incorporated as a private [exempted] company limited by shares under [companies legislation].
