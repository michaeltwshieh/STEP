## 1. Examination Arrangements

- The exam is split  into  two  parts,  Section  A  and  Section  B.  Candidates have four hours to complete both  sections. Section A consists of 20 MCQ  questions taken directly in the assessment portal. Section B consists of 5 essay questions of which you are only required to answer 4 questions; you shall be required to upload an answer booklet for Section B.
- The link to the examination paper will be clearly labelled and located in the Final Assessment section of the platform for your course.
- The format of your examination is illustrated by the Specimen Examination Papers provided on  the  online  learning  platform.  Please  review  these  carefully  ahead  of  the  examination. Details  of  the  examination  requirements  (for  example,  the  number  of  questions  to  be answered) will be included at the front of the examination paper.

## Section A

- 20 MCQs shall be answered directly in the assessment system, this section must be completed first before moving onto Section B

## Section B

- Candidates may print Section B of the examination paper or view it on screen.
- This is an open-book and non-invigilated examination.
- There is no word limit for the examination.
- Answers must be submitted via the submission portal for Section B. This can be accessed through the same link for your examination paper in the Final Assessment section on the online learning platform.
- Answers emailed to CLT International will not be accepted. Should you have any difficulty uploading your examination submission, please make contact with CLT International before the submission deadline.

- Answers can be in a word document  or  .pdf  file.  Handwritten , or  printed  and scanned answers will not be accepted. Please contact CLT International before your examination date if you have any concerns.
- You must use a cover page that includes your submission date, Customer Contact Number, the course title, and number of pages.
- Answers should be presented appropriately for black and white printing .
- You may use your Customer Contact Number as the file name when saving.
- Your name must not appear anywhere in your answer or in the file name. Please ensure you check the name of your file prior to submission and be mindful of any auto-fill options you may have set up in your browser.

## 3. Examination Protocol

- The examination is open-book . This means that you may consult your course materials and other texts or websites. However, we strictly prohibit copying and pasting large passages of text from the course materials or any other source without appropriate referencing. You are permitted to cite from course materials or any other sources to support your answers, however, please ensure that you reference any direct quotes clearly using quotation marks.

Indirect quotes or paraphrasing should also be attributed to the original source. Please note that application of knowledge is an assessment requirement,  and you are expected to interpret materials into your own words when answering essay-based examination or assignment questions. Copied content will be identifiable  and  could  lead  to  investigation under the Policy on Academic Malpractice, which may affect the mark that you are awarded for your answer.

Chatbots are a form of artificial intelligence (AI) that can generate detailed responses to requests for information. CLT International permits the use of chatbots. Large amounts of text, taken from any source, even if correctly referenced, will attract penalties since this provides no evidence of original work on your part.

- All answers must be the candidate's own original work . Consultation and/or collusion with others is strictly  prohibited. Self-plagiarism  (i.e.  use  of  content you have submitted for another examination previously, whether with CLTI or elsewhere) is strictly prohibited.
- All answers will be submitted through a plagiarism detection software and cases of suspected academic malpractice will be dealt with under the Policy on Academic Malpractice available on the online learning platform.
- By submitting an answer you are agreeing to the below declaration:

' I declare that this work is entirely my own in accordance with the STEP/CLTI Policy on Academic Malpractice.  All  external  references  and  sources  are  clearly acknowledged and identified within the contents. '

## 4. Submitting your Answers for Section B

- Answers must be submitted via the examination submission portal on the online learning platform. You will be able to find this in the Final Assessment section of the online learning platform via a link.
- You are responsible for ensuring that you submit the correct file. We recommend that you review your file prior to submission to ensure all your answers are included. If you accidentally submit an incorrect file, you should contact CLT International immediately .
- You will only be permitted to upload one file .
- Answers must be submitted by the submission deadline . Answers submitted after this time will be subject to the following penalties:

| Time of submission                                                          | Penalty applied 1   |
|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------|
| Answer submitted up to 30 minutes after the answer submission deadline      | 10% deduction       |
| Answer submitted between 31-60 minutes after the answer submission deadline | 15% deduction       |
| Answer submitted more than 60 minutes after the answer submission deadline  | Not accepted        |

Answers submitted more than 60 minutes after the answer submission deadline will not be accepted and a mark of 0 will be awarded. Penalties will be applied based on the available marks for your examination. By way of example, a 10% deduction for an examination with 100 available marks would incur a 10 mark deduction.

You will be notified by email on the day your results are released where a penalty has been applied to your overall result.

- We strongly recommend that you do not leave it too close to the answer submission deadline to  submit  your  answer .  You  should  allow  time  to  resolve  any  unexpected  problems. If technical problems prevent you from successfully submitting your work, you must contact CLT International prior to the submission deadline . Penalties cannot be removed because the network was busy around the time of the submission deadline.
