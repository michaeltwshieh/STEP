# Appendix 21A: Director's consent to be appointed as director

## Appendix 21A:

## Director's consent to be appointed as director

## CONSENT TO BE APPOINTED AS SOLE DIRECTOR UPON

## IT IS NOTED:

- (A) THAT [name of subscriber(s)] (the 'Subscriber') having subscribed to the Company and delivered the signed memorandum of association of the Company and other prescribed particulars to the registrar.
- (B) The registrar issuing a certificate of incorporation of the Company on [date].
- (C) The Subscriber proposes to appoint the undersigned person or entity as sole director of the Company.

## CONSENT TO ACT

The undersigned proposed appointee to the board:

1. HEREBY CONSENTS to his or its appointment as sole director of the Company [such consent being conditional upon the Company entering into a director's service agreement in terms that the undersigned has agreed to].
2. CONFIRMS that he or it is eligible to be appointed as director in accordance with the [relevant companies legislation].

DATE

SIGNED
