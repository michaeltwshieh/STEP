# Appendix 20B: Trustees' Resolution to Close the Trust upon the Death of Life Tenant

## Appendix 20B:

## Trustees' Resolution to Close the Trust upon the Death of Life Tenant

## [NAME OF TRUST COMPANY] Written Resolution of the Directors Of [Name] Trust Company (' the Trustee') IT IS NOTED THAT

- (A) This Resolution is made in respect of a Deed of Settlement between [Settlor] and the Trustee made on [date] known as the [Name] Trust ('the Trust' ).
- (B) The Trustee is the present trustee of the Trust and passes this resolution solely in its capacity as Trustee of such Trust.
- (C) [Name of life tenant] ('the Life Tenant' ) died on [date] and, under clause [x] of the Trust, [name] (the ' Remainderman ') is entitled to the Trust Fund held in the Trust absolutely.
- (D) The Trustee [and the Company underlying the Trust that holds the Trust Fund] has rendered trust [and company] accounts for the financial year beginning [date] calculated up to the date of death of the Life Tenant, and paid all known expenses and the Remainderman [and executors of the estate of the Life Tenant] have approved such accounts by each signing the same.
- (E) [The Trustee has advertised for claimants under [legislation of the governing law of the trust] and has not received any claim within the statutory period therefor.]
- (F) The Trustee is satisfied that the trust has been brought to an end by the death of the Life Tenant and that the Remainderman is now entitled to the Trust Fund absolutely.
- (E) The Remainderman is prepared to release and discharge the Trustee from claims relating to the administration of the Trust and to indemnify the Trustee in respect of future trust expenses, taxes and other claims that may arise in the future in the manner hereafter appearing.

## THE TRUSTEE HEREBY RESOLVES

1. THAT the Trustee hereby approves the draft Deed of Release and Indemnity attached hereto and that [name] be and is hereby authorised to execute it as a deed on behalf of the Trustee.
2. THAT the Trustee shall transfer forthwith the legal title to the various assets that comprise the Trust Fund to the Remainderman [and that the relevant trust officer be and is hereby authorised to prepare and execute on behalf of the Trustee all necessary documentation to fulfil the intent of this resolution].
3. [THAT the Trustee shall and hereby irrevocably declares that it holds shares of the Company that underlies the Trust for and on behalf of the Remainderman absolutely, freed and discharged from the terms of the Trust].
