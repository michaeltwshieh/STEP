## Appendix 6: Deed of Addition to Class of Beneficiaries

## DATE:

BY:

[Name of Settlor] (the 'Settlor')

## RECITALS

- (A) This Deed is supplemental to a Deed of Settlement dated [date] made between the Settlor and the [Name] Trust Company Ltd (the 'Trustee') and therein named as [Name] Trust (hereafter referred to as 'the Trust'). Where the context permits, defined terms and expressions in the Trust shall have the same meaning in this Deed.
- (B) The Trustee is the present trustee of the Trust.
- (C) Clause 2 of the Trust gives the Settlor the power, exercisable by deed, at any time during the Trust Period to add persons to the class of Beneficiaries.
- (D) The Settlor wishes to add the persons named in the Schedule to the class of Beneficiaries.

## OPERATIVE PROVISIONS

In exercise of the powers conferred upon him [her] by clause 2 of the Trust, the Settlor hereby DECLARES THAT , from the date of this Deed, and in respect of the whole of the Trust Fund and its income, the persons named in the Schedule shall be added to the class of Beneficiaries and shall henceforth be Beneficiaries of this Trust.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the Settlor has executed this Deed as a deed on the date stated above.
