# Appendix 30: Specimen STAR Trust for Employee Benefit Trust

## Appendix 30:

## Specimen STAR Trust for Employee Benefit Trust

## THIS DECLARATION OF TRUST

## DATED BETWEEN: [NAME OF TRUSTEE] of [address] ('the Trustee '); [NAME OF ENFORCER] of [address] ('the Enforcer '). RECITALS

- (A) This trust shall be known as the [Name] Trust ('the Trust ') and the provisions of Part VIII of the Trusts Law shall apply to it.
- (B) The Trustee has received or has otherwise had placed under its control the property defined in sub-clause 1.12(a) as the Initial Trust Fund with the intent that such property be held upon the trusts set out herein.

## OPERATIVE PROVISION

## THIS DEED WITNESSES as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

In this deed:

- 1.1 'Beneficiaries' means (a) [Name] and (b) any person or class of persons added to the class of Beneficiaries by the Trustee pursuant to clause 9.
- 1.2 'Business Plan' means the Business Plan set out on Schedule 2.
- 1.3 'Company' means [Name of employer company]
- 1.4 'Controlled Company' means any company in which the Trustees hold as part of the Trust Fund a controlling interest, namely the ownership of shares of the company that confer voting rights sufficient to elect the majority of directors.
- 1.5 'Employees' means an employee or officer, including non-executive directors, of the Company, or subsidiaries of the Company within its group.
- 1.6 'Eligible Person' means Employees and former Employees from time to time and the spouses and children of such Employees.
- 1.7 'Enforcer' means the Enforcer or any other successor enforcer for the time being of the Trust.
- 1.8 'Objects' means the objects of this Trust described in clause 3.
- 1.9 'Overriding Powers' means the powers set out in clause 7.

- 1.10 'Purposes' means the purposes of this Trust described in clause 4.
- 1.11 'Trustees' means the Trustee or trustees for the time being of this Trust.
- 1.12 'Trust Fund' means: (a) the Initial Trust Fund of $10.00; (b) all property transferred to and accepted by the Trustees to hold upon the terms of this Trust; and (c) all accumulations of income.
- 1.13 'Trust Period' means the period from the date of this deed until such date as the Trustees declare to be the termination date pursuant to clause 23, when this Trust shall be brought to an end.

## 2. Part viii of the trusts law

Part VIII of the Trusts Law (as revised from time to time) of the Cayman Islands shall apply to this Trust.

## 3. Objects

The Objects of this Trust are:

- 3.1 to apply the Trust Fund for the Purposes in accordance with Clause 4;
- 3.2 to apply the Trust Fund for the benefit of any of the Beneficiaries or the Purposes in accordance with Clauses 6 and 7; and
- 3.3 with the prior written consent of the Enforcer, to apply the Trust Fund for charitable purposes generally.

## 4. Purposes

- 4.1 The Purposes for which this Trust is established are to provide a mechanism for the control of the Controlled Company by holding the shares of the Controlled Company and exercising the rights to vote conferred upon the Trustees as holder of such shares and, in particular, to carry out the Business Plan (to enable the Controlled Company to operate independently and administer an employee share plan for the benefit of Employees of the Company).
- 4.2 The Trustees and the Enforcer jointly shall have power to resolve any uncertainty as to the Purposes or to the mode of execution of the trusts created by this Trust.
- 4.3 The Trustees shall carry out the Business Plan in dealing with the Trust Fund and in exercising its rights as holder of the shares of the Company.

## 5. Trusts of original and additional property

- 5.1 The Trustees shall hold the Trust Fund upon trust subject to the trusts, powers and provisions of this Trust.
- 5.2 The Trustees may at any time during the Trust Period accept any money investments or property of whatsoever nature and wheresoever situate to be held upon the terms of this Trust.

## 6. Trust income

During the Trust Period and subject to the Overriding Powers set out in clause 7:

- 6.1 The Trustees shall pay or apply such of the income of the Trust Fund to or for the benefit of any Beneficiaries, or for the furtherance of the Purposes, in accordance with any direction given in writing by the Enforcer.
- 6.2 The Trustees shall accumulate any or all of the income of the Trust Fund not paid or applied pursuant to clause 6.1 and such accumulated income shall be added to the capital of the Trust Fund.

## 7. Overriding powers

## 7.1 Power of appointment

- 7.1.1 Subject to the prior written consent of the Enforcer, the Trustees may declare and appoint that they hold all or any part of the Trust Fund for or for the benefit of any one or more of the Beneficiaries or for the furtherance of the Purposes on such terms as the Trustees think fit.
- 7.1.2 The exercise of the power of appointment conferred by sub-clause 7.1.1 may be revocable or irrevocable and shall be by deed.

## 7.2 Power of re-settlement

- 7.2.1 Subject to the prior written consent of the Enforcer, the Trustees may transfer all or any part of the Trust Fund to the trustees of another trust, wherever established, to hold on the terms of that trust, freed and released from the terms of this Trust, provided at least one person who may benefit is (or would if living be) a Beneficiary or if the Purposes would be furthered.
- 7.2.2 The exercise of the power of re-settlement conferred by sub-clause 7.2.1 may be revocable or irrevocable and shall be by deed.

## 8. DEFAULT CLAUSE

Subject to the powers in Clauses 6 and 7, the Trustees shall at the expiry of the Trust Period hold the Trust Fund and the income thereof on trust for any one or more of the Beneficiaries, and if more than one in such shares as the Trustees shall in their absolute discretion determine, and subject to and in default of such a determination for the Beneficiaries in equal shares absolutely; and if there shall be no Beneficiaries, for such charitable purposes as the Trustees shall in their absolute discretion determine.

## 9. ADDING BENEFICIARIES

- 9.1 Subject to the prior written consent of the Enforcer, the Trustees may at any time during the Trust Period add one or more Eligible Persons as Beneficiaries provided that no Person who has been excluded irrevocably as a Beneficiary shall be added.
- 9.2 Any such addition may be revocable or irrevocable and shall be by deed.

## 10. EXCLUDING BENEFICIARIES

- 10.1 The Enforcer may at any time during the Trust Period give a direction in writing to the Trustees to exclude one or more persons as Beneficiaries.
- 10.2 Any such exclusion may be revocable or irrevocable and shall be by deed. Any person so excluded shall for the period of his exclusion be incapable of benefiting under this Trust.

## 11. APPOINTMENT RETIREMENT AND REMOVAL OF TRUSTEES

- 11.1 The power of appointing Trustees may be exercised by the Enforcer.
- 11.2 If the Enforcer shall be unwilling or incapable of acting whether by virtue of incapacity or because he [she] cannot be found or should he [she] fail to appoint a successor Trustee within a period of 60 days of receipt of the Trustee's notice of his [her] wish to be discharged from trusteeship, then the Trustees may appoint a successor Trustee.
- 11.3 A Trustee may retire by document in writing delivered to the Enforcer. On delivery of such document the Trustee shall be discharged provided that there remains at least one Trustee.  Where on delivery of such document no Trustee shall remain in office, such document shall only take effect upon the appointment of a successor Trustee.
- 11.4 A Trustee shall cease to be a Trustee if:
- 11.4.1 the Trustee ceases to exist, is unfit to act, is declared bankrupt (or similar status) in any part of the world or makes (or seeks to make) any arrangement or composition with creditors in any part of the world, enters into liquidation or dissolution whether compulsory or voluntary (not being merely a voluntary liquidation for the purposes of amalgamation, merger, redomiciliation or re-construction);
- 11.4.2 the Trustee is removed by the Enforcer pursuant to Clause 11.5.
- 11.5 The Enforcer may remove any Trustee by deed delivered to the Trustee at any time during the Trust Period. Upon delivery of such document, the outgoing Trustee shall cease to be a Trustee and shall be discharged provided that there remains in office a continuing or successor Trustee.

## 12. Retirement and removal of Enforcer

- 12.1 The Enforcer shall cease to be an Enforcer if the Enforcer: dies or ceases to exist; becomes unfit to act; is declared bankrupt (or similar status) in any part of the world or makes (or seeks to make) any arrangement or composition with creditors in any part of the world; enters into liquidation or dissolution whether compulsory or voluntary (not being merely a voluntary liquidation for the purposes of amalgamation, merger, redomiciliation or re-construction).
- 12.2 The Enforcer shall cease to be Enforcer by giving not less than 30 days' notice in writing to the Trustees of the Enforcer's wish to retire; or is removed by the court in the jurisdiction of the governing law.

## 13. Appointment of Enforcer

- 13.1 When an Enforcer ceases to be Enforcer under Clause 12, if he [she] was the sole Enforcer then a replacement Enforcer shall be appointed by [Name] by deed delivered to the outgoing Enforcer and the Trustees.
- 13.2 If there shall at any time be no Enforcer of this Trust for a period of 21 days the Trustees shall by a document in writing appoint an Enforcer.

## 14. Investment and Administrative Provisions

- 14.1 The Trustees shall have no duty to invest the Trust Fund and may not exercise any Investment Powers other than in accordance with directions given in writing by the Enforcer.
- 14.2 The Enforcer may direct the Trustees to exercise any of their powers of investment.
- 14.3 Subject as above, the Administrative Provisions in Schedule 1 shall have effect.

## 15. Exercise of Enforcer's Powers and Trustees' Protection

- 15.1 All the powers vested in the Enforcer are personal to the Enforcer and shall not be capable of being exercised by any personal representative, successor in title or any other person on his [her] behalf.
- 15.2 The powers of the Trustees specified in clause 6 and 7 may only be exercised with the prior written consent of the Enforcer.
- 15.3 Notwithstanding the terms of any direction given in writing by the Enforcer and before exercising any powers to give effect to such direction, the Trustees may require the provision of reasonable security for liabilities whether existing, future, contingent or otherwise; and/or satisfactory AML due diligence verification.
- 15.4 If at any time the Trustees have reason to believe that the Enforcer is unwilling or unfit to act or refuses to act, the Trustees shall inform the person (if any) with the power to appoint and remove Enforcers.  If the Enforcer remains in office the Trustees may apply to the court in the jurisdiction of the governing law for the removal of the Enforcer and the appointment of a replacement Enforcer.

## 16. Enforcement of the trust

- 16.1 The Enforcer has a duty to enforce the terms of this Trust, and a duty to act responsibly with a view to the proper execution of this Trust.  No person, other than the Enforcer, shall have a right or duty to enforce this Trust.
- 16.2 The Enforcer has a duty to act in good faith and in the furtherance of the Objects and all powers of the Enforcer shall be fiduciary powers.
- 16.3 The Enforcer has no duty to supervise or investigate the administration of this Trust save that the Enforcer shall investigate any allegation of wrongdoing or unfitness on the part of the Trustees made by any director or manager of the Controlled Company.
- 16.4 The Enforcer may assume in conducting any review or investigation pursuant to sub-clause 16.3 above that information supplied by the Trustees (or any director or manager of the Controlled Company) is true and fair.

- 16.5 In deciding whether or not to commence legal proceedings concerning this Trust, or to continue, compromise or abandon such proceedings, the Enforcer may in his [her] discretion: rely upon legal or other advice obtained by the Enforcer or the Trustees; have regard to the cost, publicity and other adverse effects of the proceedings on the Trust; abandon or decline to commence legal proceedings unless funded, or assured of funding, to the Enforcer's satisfaction that all legal and other costs of the proceedings shall be paid by the Trustees out of the Trust Fund.
- 16.6 In the event that the Enforcer decides to, or investigates the potential to, commence legal proceedings against the Trustees to enforce the trusts and powers created by this Trust, the Trustees shall advance out of the Trust Fund all monies reasonably required to meet the Enforcer's costs, including any retainer required by the Enforcer's legal advisers or representatives and in making such advances the Trustees shall reply upon statements by the Enforcer's legal advisers or representatives as to the amount or amounts required.
- 16.7 The Enforcer shall be entitled to inspect and take copies of all or any documents, records or accounts relating to this Trust, the Trust Fund and the administration or investment thereof by the Trustees and the Trustees shall promptly provide the Enforcer with all information it may reasonably request concerning the Trust and its administration by the Trustees.

## 17. Trustee and enforcer remuneration

- 17.1 The Trustee may charge remuneration, payable out of the Trust Fund, in accordance with its usual terms and conditions of business in force from time to time during the Trust Period, for any services that the Trustee provides to or on behalf of the Trust.
- 17.2 A Trustee shall be entitled to reimbursement out of the Trust Fund for, or to pay out of the Trust Fund, all expenses and liabilities reasonably incurred in connection with the Trust.
- 17.3 The Enforcer may charge reasonable remuneration, payable out of the Trust Fund, for any services that he [she] provides to, on behalf of or in connection with the Trust and reimbursement for any expenses reasonably incurred.
- 17.4 The Trustee may provide banking services upon its usual terms and may retain any reasonable commission or profit in respect of any transaction relating to this Trust provided that it would be entitled in the normal course of business to receive and retain the commission or profit.

## 18. Liability of trustees and enforcers

- 18.1 The Trustee shall not be liable for loss to the Trust Fund when it has acted in accordance with a direction given in writing by the Enforcer.
- 18.2 Neither the Trustee nor the Enforcer shall be liable for loss to the Trust Fund arising from any error, mistake, act or omission to act committed by the Trustee in relation to the exercise of any of its powers unless caused by its own actual fraud, wilful misconduct or gross negligence.
- 18.3 Neither the Trustee nor the Enforcer shall be liable for loss arising from the negligence, wilful misconduct or fraud of any delegate, agent or adviser

appointed or employed by the Trustees in good faith; or anything done or omitted to be done in reliance upon any advice given or purporting to have been given by any investment adviser or manager appointed or employed by the Trustees.

## 19. Variation and rectification

- 19.1 Subject to the prior written consent of the Enforcer, the Trustees may at any time during the Trust Period by document in writing vary, amend, add to or delete any of the provisions of this Trust (including the Business Plan) as the Trustees may consider expedient for the Objects of this Trust.
- 19.2 Any such variation, amendment, addition, deletion or rectification:
- 19.2.1 shall not infringe the governing law, nor permit any Beneficiary excluded to benefit;
- 19.2.2 shall not alter or affect the rights of any Beneficiary which have accrued before the date of the variation, amendment, addition, deletion or rectification; and
- 19.2.3 shall not invalidate any previous payment or application of the Trust Fund.

## 20. Reforming the trust

If the furtherance of the Purposes becomes in whole or in part impossible or impracticable; or unlawful or contrary to public policy; or obsolete in that, by reason of changed circumstances, it fails to achieve the general intent of this Trust, the Trustees may, with the prior written consent of the Enforcer, reform the Purposes as they think fit.

## 21. Disclosure and confidentiality

- 21.1 Subject to the requirements of the governing law regarding the right of the Trustees to refuse disclosure of any document, the Trustees shall not (unless compelled to do so by an order of a court of competent jurisdiction) be bound to disclose to any person any of the following documents:
- 21.1.1 any document disclosing any deliberations of the Trustees as to the manner in which the Trustees should exercise any power or discretion conferred on them by this Trust or disclosing the reasons for any particular exercise or non-exercise of any such power or discretion;
- 21.1.2 any letter of wishes written by any person relating to the exercise of powers or discretions by the Trustees; or
- 21.1.3 any matters which are otherwise confidential or in respect of which the Trustees owe duties of confidentiality.
- 21.2 Subject to the requirements of the governing law, the Trustees shall keep the affairs of this Trust confidential and are not obliged to disclose to any Beneficiary or any other person having any interest in this Trust or any other person whatsoever any document or other matter relating to the trusts of this Trust or concerning the whole or any part of the Trust Fund.

## 22. Governing Law

The governing law of this Trust shall be the law of the Cayman Islands, the courts of which shall be the forum for the administration of this Trust. The Cayman Islands courts shall have exclusive jurisdiction in any proceedings involving rights or obligations under this Trust.

## 23. Declaration to Terminate Trust by end of Trust Period

Subject to the prior written consent of the Enforcer, the Trustees may at any time by deed declare a date on which this Trust shall terminate, which shall be the date of expiration of the Trust Period.

## 24. Irrevocability

This Trust is irrevocable.

## Schedule 2

## The Business Plan

The Business Plan of the Trust is:

1. To hold all the shares of the Controlled Company ('the Shares ') to enable the Controlled Company to operate independently and administer an employee share scheme ('the Plan ') for the benefit of the Employees of the Company.  The Trustees' role in relation to the Plan is set out in this deed and in the Trustee Terms of Engagement dated [date].
2. To exercise the rights conferred upon the holder of the Shares, inter alia, to procure the following:
3. (a) that the Trustees, as holders of the Shares, appoint or join in appointing such director or directors of the Controlled Company as the Enforcer may direct the Trustees in writing;
4. (b) that the day-to-day management of the Controlled Company and the operation and administration of the employee share Plan be left to the board of directors (or other managing board or committee of the Controlled Company or persons to whom the appropriate powers are delegated by the board of directors) and the Trustees shall not be required to make any enquiry into, determination in relation, to or take any action in respect of, or be responsible for any transaction entered into by the board of directors or other managing board or committee of the Controlled Company.
3. To consult with the Enforcer and the board of the Controlled Company with respect to the addition of any Beneficiaries of the Trust in the event that assets are to be distributed from the Controlled Company to the Trustees to be dealt with in accordance with the dispositive and administrative powers conferred on the Trustees and the Enforcer of the Trust by this deed.
4. It is contemplated that the Controlled Company shall
7. 4.1 appoint [Name] as the administrator of the Plan (the 'Administrator') pursuant to the Administration Services Agreement to be entered between the Controlled Company and the Administrator; and
8. 4.2 appoint [Name] as the investment manager ('the Investment Manager') in relation to the investment activities of the Controlled Company pursuant to an Investment Management Agreement to be entered between the Controlled Company and the Investment Manager. The Investment Manager will perform its duties to the Controlled Company in accordance with the Investment Guidelines set out therein.
5. The Controlled Company will supervise the activities, and be responsible for oversight, of the Administrator and Investment Manager.
6. The Controlled Company will be responsible, with the assistance of the Administrator and the Investment Manager, for the administration of the Plan including decisionmaking on all benefits to be conferred on Employees and the terms on which the same are conferred.

7. The Trustees shall not (without first obtaining the written consent of the Enforcer) vote at a general meeting of the Controlled Company, transfer, redeem, repurchase or otherwise dispose of or deal with the Shares of the Controlled Company.
8. In the event that the Controlled Company is wound up in accordance with its constitution and applicable law, the Trustees may thereafter apply the Trust Fund in accordance with the Purposes and those contained in this Business Plan or, following consultation with the Enforcer, re-invest the Trust Fund.
