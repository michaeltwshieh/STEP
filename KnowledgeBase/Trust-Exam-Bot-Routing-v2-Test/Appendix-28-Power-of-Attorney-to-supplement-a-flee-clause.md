## Appendix 28: Power of Attorney (to supplement a flee clause)

## DATE

BY : [Name of Trustees] ('the Trustees ')

## RECITALS

- (A) This Power of Attorney is supplemental to a Deed of Settlement dated [date] between [name] ('the Settlor ') and the Trustees known as the [name of trust] ('the Trust ').
- (B) Clause [x] of the Trust contains a flee clause, so that upon the occurrence of an Emergency Event the Trustees shall cease to be trustee of the Trust and [Name of the New Trustee] ('the New Trustee' ) shall become the new trustee of the Trust. In order to facilitate the transition of the Trust Fund to the New Trustee upon the occurrence of an Emergency Event, sub-clause [xx] of the Trust states:

The [Retiring] Trustees shall be divested of title to all the assets comprised in the Trust Fund and those assets shall vest automatically in the New Trustee (and the Trustees hereby irrevocably grants to the New Trustee a power of attorney to transfer those assets to the New Trustee on behalf of the Retiring Trustees).

- (C) The Trust Fund comprises assets held by [Name of Company], an underlying company incorporated and managed by the Trustees for the purposes of holding the Trust Fund ('the Underlying Company' ), the shares of which are held by the Trustees.
- (D) In this Deed all definitions, terms and expressions shall, where the context so permits and unless otherwise stated herein, have the meanings respectively ascribed to them by the Trust.

## OPERATIVE PROVISIONS

1. Pursuant to clause [xx] of the Trust, the Trustees hereby appoint the New Trustee to be its attorney.
2. The New Trustee is irrevocably authorised during the Trust Period to transfer the shares in the Underlying Company to itself upon the occurrence of an Emergency Event when the New Trustee becomes the new trustee of the Trust.

IN WITNESS the Trustees have duly executed this Power of Attorney as a deed on the date above.
