# Appendix 2: Sample Terms and Conditions relating to Remuneration of Trust Company

## Appendix 2:

## Sample Terms and Conditions relating to Remuneration of Trust Company

## Definitions

In this document:

- 'Trustee' means the [Name] Trust Company
- ' Trust' means the trust created by the settlor and administered by the Trustee
- ' Trust Fund' means the assets held in trust by the Trustee of the Trust.

## Remuneration

Pursuant to the remuneration clause of the Trust, the Trustee shall charge to the Trust and deduct from the Trust Fund the Acceptance Fee, Annual Fee, Additional Fee and Exit Fee (together, the 'Fees'), and reimburse its Expenses incurred in administering the Trust in accordance with these terms and conditions set out below.

## Acceptance Fee

1. The Trustee shall charge a fixed fee of USD [amount] upon being appointed as trustee (the 'Acceptance Fee'), which includes all professional services rendered and work carried out in drafting the trust instrument, submitting for approval, attendances to revise and settling revisions, drafting and settling constitutive documents and incorporating the underlying company to hold the trust assets, complying with anti-money laundering, FATCA and CRS requirements, attending to the execution of the trust instrument

## The Annual Fee

2. The Trustee shall charge an annual fee of USD [amount] [(x) per cent of the value of the Trust Fund as shown in the annual trust and underlying company accounts at the financial year end] (the 'Annual Fee') for each year or part thereof that the Trustee shall serve as Trustee of the Trust, payable annually in advance.

## Additional Fee carve-out

3. The Trustee shall charge such additional fees (the 'Additional Fees') on a time-spent basis at hourly rates specified in the Schedule hereto for all professional services rendered and work carried out in connection with the administration of the Trust Fund and other matters that require time and attention on the part of the Trustee. Such Additional Fees shall be billed quarterly by the Trustee [at a frequency determined by the Trustee] and shall be paid upon production out of the Trust Fund. Non-exhaustive examples of these matters include:
2. 3.1 dealing with and managing the investment of the Trust Fund, investor relations, and investment reviews;

- 3.2 considering and making distributions, including the drafting and settling of deeds of appointment and other deeds and the execution thereof;
- 3.3 disclosures to comply with FATCA and CRS; and
- 3.4 litigation and regulatory investigations, including instructing counsel, attendances, meetings, consideration and implementation of advice, attending dispositions and trial as witness, etc.

## Exit Fee

4. The Trustee may charge an exit fee (the 'Exit Fee') of [x] per cent of the value of the Trust Fund under the administration of the Trustee upon the Trustee's retirement or being removed from office, such value being the amount shown in the trust and company accounts prepared up to the date of retirement or removal as the case may be.

## Expenses

5. The Trustee shall reimburse itself from the Trust Fund of the Trust all reasonable expenses incurred by it and/or the directors of the underlying company holding assets for the Trust ('Expenses'), in the course of carrying out their duties under the Trust, including but not limited to:
2. 5.1 all reasonable travelling, hotel and other expenses incurred by officers of the Trustee and underlying company for attending meetings with the settlor, beneficiaries and/or their advisers, or trustee or board meetings or equivalent; and
3. 5.2 telephone, fax, printing, copying charges, which shall be charged at 3% of the value of invoices for the Annual Fee and/or time costs; and
4. 5.3 courier charges.

## Fees for directorships, acting as bankers and broker, etc

6. The Trustees, or other subsidiaries in its financial services group, may act
2. 6.1 as a director or other officer, or as agent or adviser of any company that holds assets on behalf of the Trust or is in any way connected with the Trust Fund and shall not be liable to account for any remuneration, fees or profits received by the Trustee in any such capacity.
3. 6.2 as banker, broker or other agent and may retain any brokerage or other commission received by him, her or it for any transaction carried out for the Trustees for which he/she/it is in the normal course of business allowed commission.

## Power to vary

7. The Trustee may vary the Fees and Expenses and the provisions set out in these terms and conditions by notice to the Settlor (when alive) and the Principal Beneficiaries of the Trust [and the Protector].
