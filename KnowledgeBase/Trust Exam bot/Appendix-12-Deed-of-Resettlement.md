## OPERATIVE PROVISIONS

## NOW THIS DEED WITNESSES

## 1. Re-Settlement

The Trustee, in exercise of the Power of Re-Settlement, hereby irrevocably declares that it holds the Re-Settled Fund on trust to transfer it to the trustees of the Transferee Trust, freed and released from the terms of the Trust.

5. The Deed is drafted as a one-party declaration by the trustee alone in accordance with the terms of a conventional discretionary trust, similar to Example 8, in section 7 of the study manual. If the terms of the trust state that the exercise of the power requires the settlor's, or protector's consent, the settlor, or protector should be joined as a party. If, in consideration for making the resettlement, the Trustee requires an express release from liability similar to clause 4.1 of the Deed of Appointment in Appendix 9, the Beneficiaries of the Trust should be joined as parties. If the Trustee requires an express indemnity similar to clause 4.2 of the Deed of Appointment in Appendix 9, the trustee of the Transferee Trust should be joined as a party.

## 2. Addition to the Transferee Trust

The Re-Settled Fund shall be held by the trustees of the Transferee Trust upon the trusts and subject to the powers and provisions contained in the Transferee Trust as an addition to, and as one fund for all purposes with, the trust fund as defined in the Transferee Trust.

## 3. Trustee's lien

The Re-Settled Fund shall be transferred to the trustee of the Transferee Trust, or to a company under control, subject to the lien in favour of the Trustee for tax and other liabilities.

## 4. Provisions of the Trust continue to apply

The trusts, powers and provisions contained in the Trust shall continue to apply to the Trust Fund.

## 5. Payment of tax and expenses

Any tax, costs and expenses and other liabilities occasioned by the exercise of the Power of Re-Settlement shall be borne by the Re-Settled Fund.
