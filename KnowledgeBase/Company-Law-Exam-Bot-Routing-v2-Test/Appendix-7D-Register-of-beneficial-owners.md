Which condition(s) the beneficial owner meets 27

27. The definition of beneficial owner (what British call 'persons with significant control' 'PSC') is set out in applicable local companies legislation and must satisfy OECD recommendations. In England the statutory provisions are found in a new Pt 21A of the Companies Act 2006 (inserted by SBEEA) which states that a PSC meets one or more of the following conditions: (a) directly or indirectly holds more than 25% of the shares; (b) directly or indirectly holds more than 25% of the voting rights;(c) directly or indirectly holds the right to appoint and remove directors;(d) has the right to exercise, or actually exercises, significant influence or control. It is the final condition that is the killer. Control means that the PSC can direct the company's activities; influence means that the PSC can ensure, in fact, if not in law, that the company adopts the activities the PSC desires.
