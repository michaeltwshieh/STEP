# Appendix 31A: Director's resolution to apply for voluntary striking off

## Appendix 31A:

## Director's resolution to apply for voluntary striking off

## AB LIMITED RESOLUTION OF THE SOLE DIRECTOR OF AB LIMITED

## (the 'Company')

Pursuant to art.[x] of the Company's Articles of Association, we the undersigned sole director of the Company for the time being:

## NOTE :

- (A) THAT having made appropriate investigations, the director confirms the company has no assets or liabilities.
- (B) THAT the company meets the statutory conditions set out in [companies legislation] to apply for voluntary striking off.

## RESOLVE :

1. THAT the Company shall apply to the registrar for its voluntary striking off from the register.
2. THAT the sole director shall complete and sign the appropriate application form [statutory declaration of compliance] and file it with the registrar to make the said application.
