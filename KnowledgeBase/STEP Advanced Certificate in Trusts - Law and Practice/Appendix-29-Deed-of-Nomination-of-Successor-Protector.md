## Appendix 29:

## Deed of Nomination of Successor Protector

## DATE:

## PARTIES:

1. [Name] ('the Appointor ')
2. [Name] ('the Successor Protector' )
3. [Name] ('the Trustee ')

## RECITALS

- (A) This deed is supplemental to a Deed of Settlement dated [date] between [name] ('the Settlor ') and the Trustee known as the [name of trust] ('the Trust ').
- (B) The Appointor is the Protector as defined in the Trust.
- (C) By clause [x] of the Trust the Appointor has the power to nominate a person to become the Protector upon the Appointor ceasing to be the Protector.
- (D) The Appointor now wishes to exercise the power of nomination.

## OPERATIVE PROVISIONS

## 1. Nomination of Successor Protector

In exercise of the power conferred by clause [x] of the Trust, the Appointor hereby nominates the Successor Protector to become the Protector upon the Appointor ceasing to be the Protector.

## 2. Consent to nomination

The Successor Protector hereby consents to this nomination.

## 3. Power of revocation

The Appointor shall have power at any time whilst Protector, by deed, to revoke this nomination and, by the same or any other deed, to nominate any other person to be Protector under the terms of clause [x] of the Trust.

## 4. Notice of nomination

The Trustee hereby acknowledges notice of this nomination.
