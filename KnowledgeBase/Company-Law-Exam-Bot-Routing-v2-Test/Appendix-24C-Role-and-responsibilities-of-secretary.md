# Appendix 24C: Role and responsibilities of secretary

## Appendix 24C:

## Role and responsibilities of secretary

## AB LIMITED (Company [number]) ROLE AND DESCRIPTION OF RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE SECRETARY GENERAL ROLE

The secretary shall ensure that the conduct of the business of the company and its administration complies with [companies legislation], the provisions of the articles of association and other legal requirements, including in relation to public filings and record obligations; and to support the board in ensuring that the assets and business of the company are managed in a proper, efficient and appropriate manner.

## RESPONSIBILITIES

The general responsibilities of the secretary are:

1. to ensure that statutory registers are kept and maintained
2. to convene general meetings when instructed by the board or members, prepare and serve notices
3. to prepare agendas and other documents for general meetings, and ensure they are circulated
4. to ensure necessary arrangements are made for general meetings
5. to take minutes of general meetings of members, to prepare and circulate draft minutes, ensuring approved minutes are signed by the chairman and filed
6. to make necessary public filings of special resolutions
7. to convene directors' meetings when instructed by the board and ensure that proper notice is given
8. to ensure that agendas for board meetings are prepared and circulated
9. to take minutes of board meetings, to prepare and circulate draft minutes, ensuring approved minutes are signed by the chairman and filed
10. to deal with allotments and transfers of shares as instructed by the board
11. to ensure all necessary filings are made as instructed by the board in respect of directorships and beneficial owners
12. to make required filings as instructed to comply with FATCA and CRS
13. to administer generally the company as secretary
14. to ensure that relevant legal requirements are drawn to the attention of the board and complied with
15. to comply with the reasonable instructions given by the board provided compliance is in accordance with the general law and best practice.
