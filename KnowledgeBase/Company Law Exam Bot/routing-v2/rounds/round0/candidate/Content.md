# Content Index - Authoritative Section A and Section B Source Map

This file is the single authoritative legal routing map for both exam sections. It identifies sources to inspect; it does not state law. Open every selected passage before answering. Do not copy legal mapping into `routing-core.md`, `section-a.md`, `section-b.md` or `CLAUDE.md`.

## Map contract

- Course manuals and appendices are the only substantive course-law sources.
- Routes are additive candidates until final route selection is completed.
- Conditional overlays activate only on their stated facts.
- Examination attachments use a separate namespace from course appendices.
- Source warnings preserve internal gaps or conflicts; they do not authorize outside law.

## Additive issue map

### A. Company characteristics, incorporation and foreign companies

- **Sources of company law; mandatory/permissive/default statutory rules; opt-in or
  opt-out provisions; legislation versus memorandum/articles; course scope**
  - Base: Module 1 §§1-4.
  - Actual legislation and the company's actual constitution govern; model articles
    add only if named/applicable or used for an explicit comparison.
  - Detailed tax, AML/CFT, FATCA, CRS, economic-substance and regulated-service-provider
    law is outside this manual's main scope. Use only the high-level material actually
    supplied in Modules 1, 3 and 11 and flag any remaining gap.
  - Trust-and-underlying-company structures: add Modules 2 §5, 6 for funding/
    distributions and 11 for service-provider/beneficial-owner control as raised.

- **Definition of a company; company versus partnership; corporate history; development
  of offshore legislation**
  - Base: Module 2 §§1-3; Appendix 2 for the comparison table.
  - If the question asks which vehicle to form: add Module 3 §3.2 and Appendices 6A-6B.
  - If financing or limited liability is applied to a transaction: add Module 6 §3 or
    the relevant equity section.

- **Separate legal personality; Salomon; corporate ownership; limited liability;
  perpetual succession; transferability; separation of ownership and control**
  - Base: Module 2 §§4.1-4.6.
  - Veil lifting, fraud, sham, agency, parent liability or evasion: add Appendix 3 as
    reference material, not as a drafting precedent.
  - Shareholder loan or shareholder-creditor status: add Module 6 §§3 and 7.1.
  - Beneficial owner exercising management control: add Modules 7 §2, 8 §§2-3 and
    11 §3 as the facts require.

- **Use of offshore companies; asset-holding company; IBC/BC; public/private;

  - Choosing or incorporating the vehicle: Module 3 §§3.2.1-3.2.5.
  - Constitutional consequences: Module 5 §§2.3, 2.5 and 3.3-3.5.
  - Do not assume every jurisdiction retains a public/private distinction.

- **Promoter; promoter's duty, profit, fee or expenses; pre-incorporation contract;

## Appendix decision map

The appendix list is not a list of documents that can all be reproduced. Use the
classification below:

- **Model constitution** - use only if it governs or the question asks for it; the
  actual constitution prevails.
- **Reference** - use to analyse/reword; never reproduce it as a drafted document.
- **Template** - a register, checklist, table or example requiring reconstruction and
  local-law checking.
- **Precedent** - preserve its applicable structure and operative effect after the
  question, governing law/articles and facts have been applied.
- **Extract/incomplete** - never invent the omitted provisions.

For every drafting request verify: actor; document type; decision method; transaction
stage; jurisdiction/regime; operative parts; alternatives; attachments; execution
block. A mismatch requires reconsideration of the appendix.

## High-risk distinctions

- Incorporation is not foreign-company registration; foreign-company registration is
  not migration; migration is not public/private re-registration; an office move is
  not migration.
- Corporate capacity/objects are not the same as a director's authority; external
  enforceability does not erase internal director liability.
- Allotment is not issue; issue is not transfer; transfer is not transmission; legal-
  title transfer is not beneficial-only transfer.
- Authorised capital, issued capital, share premium/capital surplus, retained earnings
  and shareholder debt are distinct accounts with different consequences.
- Traditional dividend (19A+19B), director-only dividend (19C), solvency distribution
  (20E) and shareholder-loan repayment (20B+20D) are four different routes.
- Nominee shareholder is a recognised contractual role; `nominee director` does not
  remove the ordinary duties of a director.
- Company secretary, registered agent, foreign-company authorised representative,
  investment adviser/manager, attorney and meeting proxy are different offices/roles.
- Meeting notice, proxy, corporate representative, meeting minutes and certified filing
  copy are separate documents; one does not silently supply the others.
- Creation/approval/registration of security is separate from enforcement by receiver.
- Members' voluntary winding-up, creditors' voluntary winding-up, compulsory winding-
  up and striking off have different solvency assumptions, actors and documents.

## Known source and index warnings

These warnings preserve course-material inconsistencies instead of silently correcting
them with outside law. When one is material, inspect the source and flag it on
`Verify:`.

- Appendix 7C is the charge-register file but its internal heading incorrectly says
  `REGISTER OF MEMBERS`.
- Appendix 15C ends with a stray heading belonging to the following appendix; do not
  reproduce it.
- Appendix 21G's full title is `Member's resolution instructing board to enter specific
  transaction`; it also ends with a stray heading from Appendix 22.
- Appendix 25E's full title ends `specific resolution`.
- Appendix 25H concerns appointment of a `corporate representative for AGM (and
  generally)`, and Appendix 25J is notice of appointment of that corporate
  representative. Neither appoints a corporate director.
- Appendix 20C's shortened source title says `pro note`; its operative instrument is a
  promissory note.
- Appendix 18C/18D cite art.8.2, while Module 6 routes approval/refusal to art.8.3 and
  notice to art.8.5. Use the actual governing articles and flag the discrepancy.
- Appendix 27B is skeletal and says `etc., as above`; it is not a complete declaration.
- Appendix 28 expressly contains extracts and omits the substantive text of clauses
  6-15. Do not draft the missing clauses.
- Appendix 30G is a skeletal converted account requiring reconstruction and actual
  figures; it is not the approval resolution in Appendix 30F.
- Module 5 footnote 97 points to Module 9 §2.8; the relevant resolutions/voting section
  is Module 10 §2.8.
- Module 3's first-board discussion points to Module 9 §1.3.2(viii); the minutes item is
  under the Module 9 §1.4.2 powers list.
- Module 8's struck-off-company discussion points generally to Module 12 §5; restoration
  after striking off is Module 12 §6.3, whereas §5 concerns reinstatement after
  completed liquidation.
- Module 10 gives a typical 10% member-requisition example while Appendix 25B uses 15%.
  The governing statute/articles control.
- Module 10 §5.9 says directors may use a board written resolution to change the
  articles when they lack share-issue authority. That conflicts with Module 6 §1.2.2
  and Appendix 16A's member-authorisation route. Follow the governing statute and
  actual articles; do not let the board amend articles unless they validly confer that
  power.
- Module 10 §5.3 refers to surrender of the `transferee's` share certificate, whereas
  Module 6 §5.1 and Appendix 18B use the old certificate associated with the
  transferor's holding for cancellation. Follow the internally consistent Module 6/
  Appendix 18 chain and actual records, and flag the wording conflict if material.
- Module 10 contains tension over whether the 2008 Model Articles supply an alternate-
  director provision. Inspect the actual articles; do not infer a universal right.
- Module 6 and Appendices 18C/18D contain the transfer-article mismatch noted above;
  Module 6 also contains source tensions concerning Gibraltar private/public share-
  premium treatment and the BVI distribution-definition pinpoint. Keep the regime and
  exact course passage visible.
- Module 12 is internally inconsistent on whether the Bahamas has a statutory voluntary
  striking-off route. Flag the conflict rather than resolving it externally.
- Module 3 contains incomplete/stray cross-reference text near its footnotes, Module 11
  contains a `Task 10.1` numbering artefact and Module 9's source heading says `corporates
  documents`. These are editorial artefacts, not new legal categories.


- The appendix sequence intentionally jumps from 25H to 25J; Appendix 25I does not exist.
