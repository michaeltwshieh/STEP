## AB LIMITED (IN MEMBERS' VOLUNTARY WINDING UP) RESOLUTION OF THE MEMBERS OF AB LIMITED (the 'Company')

Pursuant to Article [x] of the Company's Articles of Association, we the undersigned sole member of the Company for the time being:

## NOTE :

- (A) THAT there is produced and attached hereto the Liquidator's unaudited Statement of Account for the period [date] to [date], showing the manner in which the winding up of the Company has been conducted and the property of the Company disposed of.

## RESOLVE :

1. THAT the Liquidator's Statement of Account be and is hereby received and approved.
2. THAT the amount of $[amount], remaining after payment in full of the debts and liabilities and the costs of the liquidation be applied firstly to repay the members the amounts paid up on the shares held by them, and that the balance be then distributed among the members in proportion to the number of shares held by them respectively.
3. THAT the books, accounts and papers of the Company and the Liquidators be retained by the Liquidators for a period of six years from the date of dissolution of the Company, and thereafter destroyed by them.
