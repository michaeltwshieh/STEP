## Appendix 24A: Trustees' Resolution to Bring, Defend Legal Proceedings

## [NAME OF TRUST COMPANY]

## WRITTEN RESOLUTION OF THE DIRECTORS OF [NAME] TRUST COMPANY (' the Trustee') IT IS NOTED THAT

- (A) This Resolution is made in respect of a Deed of Settlement between [Settlor] and the Trustee made on [date] known as the [Name] Trust ('the Trust' ).   The Trustee is the present trustee of the Trust and passes this resolution solely in its capacity as Trustee of such Trust.
- (B) Clause [x] of the Trust confers upon the Trustee a power to bring legal proceedings to enforce claims relating to the Trust and/or the Trust Fund.
- (C) Based upon the advice of counsel, the Trustee believes it has a valid claim against [Name] (the 'Defendant') arising from [describe concisely the cause of action] (the 'Claim').

## IT IS RESOLVED

1. THAT in exercise of the power conferred by clause [x] of the Trust, the Trustee shall pursue the Claim, through legal proceedings if necessary.
2. THAT [Name] Attorneys [and Her Majesty's Counsel] be and are hereby instructed, upon such fee arrangements as may be acceptable to the Trustee, to file legal proceedings against the Defendant to enforce the Claim.
3. THAT officers of the Trustee be and are authorised to continue [further] negotiations with the Defendant with a view to agreeing a compromise settlement to the Claim which is acceptable to the Trustee.
4. THAT any actions taken by trust officers to the date of these resolutions in connection with the Claim be and are hereby ratified and approved as acts of the Trustee.
