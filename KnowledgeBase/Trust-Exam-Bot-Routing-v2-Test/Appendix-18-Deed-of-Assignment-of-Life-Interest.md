# Appendix 18: Deed of Assignment of Life Interest

## Appendix 18:

## Deed of Assignment of Life Interest

## DATE:

## PARTIES:

- (1) [Name of life tenant] ('the Assignor' ); and
- (2) [Name of Assignee] (the ' Assignee' )
- (3) [Name of Trust Company] ('the Trustee ').

## RECITALS

- (A) This Deed is supplemental to a Deed of Settlement made on [date] between the Settlor and the Trustee and known as the [Name] Trust ('the Trust ').
- (B) The Trustee is the present trustee of the Trust.
- (C) Under the Trust, the Assignor is the life tenant under the Trust and is entitled to the income of the Trust Fund for her [his] life.
- (D) The Assignor wishes to assign her [his] interest in the income of the Trust Fund to the Assignee.
- (E) The Trustee is a party to this Deed to acknowledge that it has received notice thereof.
- (F) In this Deed the definition of 'Trust Fund' shall have the same meaning as in the Trust.

## OPERATIVE PROVISIONS

1. The Assignor hereby assigns to the Assignee absolutely the Assignor's interest in the Trust.
2. The Assignor promises to provide such evidence of life from time to time as may reasonably be required.
3. The Trustee acknowledges that the assignment has been made and effected.
