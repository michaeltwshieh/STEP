# Appendix 10: Deed of Appointment upon New Trusts

## Appendix 10:

## Deed of Appointment upon New Trusts

## DATE:

- BY: [Name and address of trust company] ('the Trustee ').

## RECITALS

- (A) This Deed is supplemental to a Deed of Settlement between [Settlor] and the Trustee made on [date] known as the [Name] Trust ('the Trust' ).
- (B) The Trustee is the present trustee of the Trust.
- (C) By clause [x] of the Trust, the Trustee is conferred with a discretionary power of appointment of the income and capital of the Trust Fund in the following terms: [reproduce the clause from the trust instrument]
- (D) [Name of beneficiary] (the 'Life Tenant' and the children of the Life Tenant are members of the class of Beneficiaries of the Trust.
- (E) The Trustee wishes to exercise the power of appointment conferred by clause [x] of the Trust in respect of the funds specified in the Schedule (the ' Appointed Fund' ) in the manner hereafter set out.
- (F) In this Deed all definitions, terms and expressions shall, where the context so permits and unless otherwise stated herein, have the meanings respectively ascribed to them by the Trust.

## OPERATIVE PROVISIONS

## NOW THIS DEED WITNESSES

## 1. Appointment

The Trustee, in exercise of the power of appointment conferred by clause [x] of the Trust, hereby irrevocably appoints and declares that the Appointed Fund shall henceforth be held by the Trustee upon the following trusts:

- 1.1 The income of the Appointed Fund shall be paid to the Life Tenant during her [his] lifetime;
- 1.2 [The Trustees may, at any time during the Trust Period, pay or apply the whole or any part of the Appointed Fund to her [him] for her [his] advancement or benefit in such manner as the Trustees may, in their discretion, think fit. In exercising the powers conferred by this sub-clause, the Trustees shall have regard only to the interests of the Life Tenant and to disregard all other interests or potential interests in the Appointed Fund.]

- 1.3 Upon the death of the Life Tenant, the capital and income of the Appointed Fund shall be held upon trust for the children of the Life Tenant as attain the age of 21 years before the end of the Trust Period, and if more than one in equal shares, absolutely.

## 2. Application of Trust provisions

The trusts, powers and provisions contained in the Trust shall continue to apply to the Appointed Fund so far as consistent with the provisions of this deed.

## 3. Payment of tax and expenses

Any tax, costs and expenses and other liabilities occasioned by the appointment shall be borne by the Appointed Fund.

## 4. Terms of the Trust to stand

Subject to the terms of this deed, the Trust shall stand. 3

## EXECUTION

IN WITNESS whereof the Trustee has executed and delivered this Deed of Appointment as a deed on the date stated at the beginning of it.
