# Old Section B Goal - Root-Cause Record

## Outcome carried forward

The old goal produced a useful Section B routing system but did not meet its frozen
zero-critical-error activation gate after three repair rounds. The candidate was not
activated. The new shared-routing goal reuses the verified assets and treats the old
rounds as root-cause evidence, not as new repair rounds.

## Verified completed work

- Frozen baseline, 23-case corpus, nine minimal pairs, question-only fixtures and hash
  locks exist.
- Static checks passed for 12 module files, 90 uniquely labelled appendices, filename
  resolution, Markdown/link integrity and unchanged source materials.
- Source precedence, examination/course appendix namespaces, six locks, fact
  disposition, independent lifecycle retrieval, branch isolation, hard relevance and
  materials-gap handling were implemented.
- Material-fact disposition reached 107/107 in all three repair rounds and forbidden
  routes remained zero.
- The Q2 corporate-authority/document architecture was materially repaired: separate
  upstream nominee and corporate-director authority, Company member sanction,
  Company board approval, exhibits, execution and records stages.

## Frozen-oracle corrections for the new suite

These changes are source corrections, not candidate-driven relaxation:

1. **S01:** Appendix 25A is a checked procedural companion, not a component of the
   requested notice. Score the notice against Appendix 25E and applicable Appendix 30B
   components; preserve the unresolved liquidation regime.
2. **P05B:** use `18A + (18B XOR 18C+18D)`, selected only by the actual articles/board
   decision.
3. **P08A:** use `30G + (30E XOR 30F)`, because Module 12 describes the written approval
   as in lieu of the final meeting.
4. **P08B:** preserve the lack of exact creditors' voluntary liquidation precedents and
   score controlled semantic adaptation rather than exact-form transplantation.

The earlier unresolved-branch denominator correction from 34 to 31 remains valid.

## Architectural response

The shared candidate therefore uses one compact route dossier, adds a deliverable-scope
classification before lifecycle expansion, records mutually exclusive document sets,
and applies source-removal only after relevance and oracle sanity. It retains the
verified old mechanisms and does not encode specimen names or facts.
