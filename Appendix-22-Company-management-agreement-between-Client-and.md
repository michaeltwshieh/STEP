# Appendix 22: Company management agreement between Client and Administrator

## Appendix 22: and Administrator

## COMPANY MANAGEMENT [AND REGISTERED OFFICE] AGREEMENT

## THIS AGREEMENT DATED BETWEEN

[Name and address of client] (the 'Client') of the first part, and

[Name and address of the service provider] (the 'Administrators') of the second part.

## RECITALS

The Client has engaged the Administrator and the Administrator has agreed to incorporate a new company ('the Company') on behalf of the Client and to manage the same in accordance with the provisions of this Agreement.

## IT IS HEREBY AGREED

## 1. Appointment of corporate administrators

The Client hereby appoints the Administrators, and the Administrators agree to act as service provider and corporate administrator of the Company upon the terms and subject to the provisions of this Agreement.

## 2. Duties

The Administrator shall incorporate the Company and, during the period of their appointment as administrator thereof under this Agreement, the Administrators shall, subject to the overall direction of the directors of the Company, conduct on behalf of the directors, all the routine day-to-day administration of the Company [and act as Secretary to/Registered Agent of the Company] and, in particular, the Administrators shall:

- 2.1 provide such office accommodation, secretarial staff and other facilities as may be required for the purpose of fulfilling their duties under this Agreement and also provide an address in [jurisdiction] to be used as the registered office of the Company and for service of process;
- 2.2 provide the company secretary [registered agent] for the Company;

- 2.3 keep and maintain the statutory registers and such records as are required by law for the proper conduct of the Company's business affairs;
- 2.4 make all necessary filings to ensure the Company is and remains in good standing;
- 2.5 provide nominees, as required, in whose names the shares in the company shall be registered;
- 2.6 dispatch to the Client (and to the auditors, if any) such notices, reports, financial statements and other written material as may be reasonably requested by them from time to time;
- 2.7 at any time during business hours, permit any duly appointed agent or representative of the Company, at the expense of the Company, to inspect any documents or records kept by the Administrators, and give such agent or representative all information and assistance they may reasonably require;
- 2.8 keep and control the use of the seal of the Company, when applicable;
- 2.9 maintain accounting records for, and produce annual financial statements of, the Company;
- 2.10 provide such other facilities and perform such other services as may be agreed from time to time between the Administrators and the Company.

## 3. Delegation

In performing their obligations under this Agreement, the Administrator may delegate such of their duties and functions to agents as they in their discretion think fit, and may consult with independent counsel, accountants and any other persons to be selected and employed by them.

## 4. Fees and expenses

[Provision for payment of the professional fees of the Administrators], including reimbursement of:

- 4.1 the out-of-pocket expenses of the Administrators (including travel and associated expenses) incurred in convening or attending meetings of the directors, calling or attending any extraordinary general meetings of the company, preparing and forwarding any notice or statement (other than notice of the annual general meeting) and all other like expenses
- 4.2 the costs of telex, facsimile, telephone calls and Email properly incurred in the course of carrying out their duties
- 4.3 fees and expenses of accountants (including auditors), lawyers and other professional advisers of the Company
- 4.4 all governmental charges, fees or expenses expressly authorised by this agreement or necessarily incurred by the Administrators in the provision of services under this agreement.

## 5. Termination

- 5.1 The Client may terminate this agreement on giving not less than six months written notice to  the Administrators.

- 5.2 The Administrators may terminate this agreement on giving not less than six months written notice to the Client except:
- i. where the Client is in breach of this Agreement or
- ii. where circumstances occur that, in the sole opinion of the Administrators, render the continued provision or performance of services, facilities and/or duties impracticable or undesirable (and in this context 'undesirable' includes the breach by the Company of any penal, criminal or fiscal legislation or the doing of any unlawful act); in any such case the Administrators may terminate the agreement on giving immediate written notice to the Client.
- 5.3 Upon termination, the Administrators shall be entitled to their fees pro rated to the date of termination, and all moneys due from the Client to the Administrators and all other liabilities of the Client under this agreement shall be paid or discharged immediately.
- 5.4 The Administrators shall, on the termination of their appointment, deliver to any succeeding  corporate service providers all books of account, registers, correspondence and records relating to the affairs of the Company that are in their possession, but the Administrators may retain such copies as they may think fit.

## 6. Lien

The Administrators shall be indemnified by the Client in respect of fees properly charged and expenses incurred in accordance with the provisions of this Agreement and while any such fees and expenses remain unpaid, the Administrators shall have a lien over any papers books or records belonging to the Company in the possession of the Administrators.

## 7. Exclusion and indemnity

## 7.1 Exclusion of liability

The Administrators shall not be liable for any loss or damage suffered by the Client arising directly or indirectly out of any error of judgement, oversight or mistake made by the Administrators in good faith in the performance of their duties under this agreement. The Administrators shall not, in the absence of fraud, wilful default [or gross negligence], be responsible for any loss or damage that the Client may suffer as the result of the performance of this Agreement.

## 7.2 Indemnity

The Client shall [jointly and severally] indemnify the Administrators against all claims and demands, including claims, judgments, costs and expenses, arising in relation to the performance of this Agreement in respect of any loss or damage sustained by any third party, otherwise than by reason of the fraud, wilful default [or gross negligence] of the Administrators.

## 8. Control

The Administrators may, at any time, do or refrain from doing any act, without reference to the Company or the Client, if in their absolute discretion, they deem it necessary or desirable to do so. The Administrators shall be entitled to refuse to carry out or participate in any act or omission that is unlawful (and it shall be in the sole determination of the Administrators whether any act or omission is or is not, unlawful). For this purpose, the Administrators may, at the expense of the Company or the Client, seek and obtain the opinion of lawyers or others. No liability shall attach to the Administrators for refusing to do or participate in any unlawful act or omission. Any expenses incurred by the Administrators in obtaining any opinion shall be recoverable from the Company or the Client on demand.

## 9. Jurisdiction

This agreement is governed by and is to be construed according to the law of [jurisdiction] and the parties submit to the jurisdiction of the courts of [jurisdiction].

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the parties hereto have executed this agreement, intending it to be effective on the following date.
