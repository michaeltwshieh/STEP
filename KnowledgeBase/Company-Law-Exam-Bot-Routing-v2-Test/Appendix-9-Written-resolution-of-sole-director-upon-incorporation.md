## Appendix 9:

## Written resolution of sole director upon incorporation

## AB LIMITED

## Written Resolutions of the Director(s) of AB LIMITED ('the Company')

Pursuant to the provisions of Article \_\_\_\_\_\_ of the Company's Articles of Association, we the undersigned sole director of the Company for the time being, hereby note and resolve as follows:

## IT IS NOTED:

## (A) Incorporation

THAT the Company's memorandum and articles of association have been registered with the Registrar of Companies and that a Certificate of Incorporation has been issued dated [date].

## (B) Subscriber shares

THAT the shares which the subscriber has agreed in the memorandum to take up shall be issued to Service Provider (Nominees) Limited.

## (C) Directors

THAT the subscribers to the memorandum have appointed [name and address] as the first and sole director of the Company.

THAT the said appointee has consented to act.

THAT it is anticipated that the Company and the appointee will enter into a directors service agreement in the form attached hereto marked 'AB1' (the 'Director's Service Agreement')

THAT the following interests have been declared and are hereby noted: [there is a common owner of the shares in the corporate subscriber, the registered shareholder holder, the registered agent and the sole corporate director of the Company].

## (D) Registered agent

THAT a copy of the written consent signed by Service Provider (Registered Agent) Ltd has been produced, confirming its consent to act as registered agent of the Company.

## IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED:

## 1. Director's Service Agreement

THAT the Director's Service Agreement be and is hereby approved and that the secretary [registered agent] is hereby authorised to execute it on behalf of the Company.

## 2. Secretary (and other officers)

THAT [name], having consented to act, be and is hereby appointed as the first Secretary of the Company [and THAT the powers be conferred, and duties imposed upon, the Secretary that are set out in the attached document entitled Role and Responsibilities of the Secretary. 28 ]

## 3. Registered agent

THAT [name] be and is hereby appointed as the registered agent of the Company.

## 4. Common Seal

THAT the seal, an impression of which is affixed in the margin of these written minutes, be and is hereby adopted as the common seal of the Company [which seal shall be affixed to any document, deed or instrument in the presence of the sole director [and registered agent] who should testify by their signatures on the sealed document that the seal has been properly affixed].

## 5. Bank account

THAT a US dollar current/checking account be opened with [name] bank and that the authorised signatures thereto be [name] and [name].

THAT the resolutions contained in the Bank's printed forms of resolutions, a copy of which is attached to and deemed part of these resolutions marked 'AB2' , be and are hereby adopted in full.

## 6. Brokerage account

THAT a brokerage and custody account be opened with [name of broker] and that the authorised signatures thereto be [name] and [name].

THAT the resolutions contained in the broker's printed forms of resolutions, a copy of which is attached to and deemed part of these resolutions marked 'AB3' , be and are hereby adopted in full.

THAT [name] be and is hereby appointed as investment advisor or attorney] at fees and remuneration to be fixed by the board.
