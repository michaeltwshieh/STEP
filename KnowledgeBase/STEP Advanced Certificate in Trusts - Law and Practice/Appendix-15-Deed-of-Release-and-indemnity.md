## OPERATIVE PROVISIONS

## NOW THIS DEED WITNESSES

## 1. Acknowledgment of receipt

The Beneficiary hereby acknowledges receipt of the Distribution.

## 2. Payment of tax and expenses

Any tax, costs and expenses and other liabilities occasioned by the Distribution shall be borne by the Beneficiary.

## 3. Trustee's lien

Nothing in this Deed shall prejudice or impair in any way any lien or charge to which the Trustee is entitled in respect of any liabilities for which it may be or become accountable.

## 4. Release and Indemnity of the Trustee

## 4.1 Release

The Beneficiary releases the Trustee entirely from all claims, demands, actions, proceedings and accounts of any kind which he [she] may have, or which any person actually or prospectively interested under the Trust in the Trust Fund or the income of the Trust Fund or the trusts of the Trust may have  for any act or thing done or omitted in the execution or purported execution of such trusts other than and except only actions arising from any fraud, wilful misconduct or dishonesty on the part of the Trustee.

## 4.2 Indemnity

[The Beneficiary promises the Trustee to keep it fully and effectually indemnified entirely from and against all existing, future or contingent liabilities, claims, demands, actions, proceedings and accounts of any kind (and any costs and expenses incurred in connection therewith) which may be brought or threatened to be brought by any person against the Trustee in connection with the Trust or any instruments or deeds supplemental thereto including this Deed or in any way relating to the Trust Fund or any act or thing done by the Trustee arising directly or indirectly or omitted in the execution of the trusts of the Trust and such indemnity shall extend to any claims or demands for taxes, duties or other fiscal liabilities payable in any part of the world in respect of the Trust Fund whether or not such claims or demands are legally enforceable against the Trustee in its jurisdiction of residence PROVIDED ALWAYS THAT:

- (1) such indemnity shall not operate to indemnify the Trustee for liabilities it would not have been entitled to an indemnity for under the terms of the Trust;
- (2) the liability of the Beneficiary under this indemnity shall be limited to the value of the amount of the Distribution.]

## EXECUTION

IN WITNESS whereof the parties have executed and delivered this Deed of Appointment as a deed on the date stated at the beginning of it.
