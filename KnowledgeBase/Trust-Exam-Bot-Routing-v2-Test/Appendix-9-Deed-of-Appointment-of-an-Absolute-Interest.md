## Deed of Appointment of an Absolute Interest

## PARTIES:

## RECITALS

- (A) This Deed is supplemental to a Deed of Settlement between [Settlor] and the Trustee made on [date] known as the [Name] Trust ('the Trust' ).
- (B) The Trustee is the present trustee of the Trust.
- (C) By clause [x] of the Trust, the Trustee is conferred with a discretionary power of appointment of the income and capital of the Trust Fund in the following terms: [reproduce the clause from the trust instrument]
- (D) The Beneficiary is a member of the class of Beneficiaries of the Trust.
- (E) The Trustee wishes to exercise the power of appointment conferred by clause [x] of the Trust by making an absolute appointment to the Beneficiary of the funds specified in the Schedule (the ' Appointed Fund' ) in the manner hereafter set out.
- (F) [The Trustee shall hold the Appointed Fund for and on behalf of the Beneficiary and the Beneficiary has agreed to release and indemnify the Trustee in the manner hereafter set out.] 1
- (G) In this Deed all definitions, terms and expressions shall, where the context so permits and unless otherwise stated herein, have the meanings respectively ascribed to them by the Trust.

## OPERATIVE PROVISIONS

## NOW THIS DEED WITNESSES

## 1. Appointment

The Trustee, in exercise of the power of appointment reserved by clause [x] of the Trust, hereby irrevocably appoints and declares that the Appointed Fund shall henceforth be held by the Trustee for the Beneficiary absolutely, freed and discharged from the terms of the Trust.

1. The Beneficiary is not required to give an express release and indemnity in consideration for the receipt of a distribution. A form of indemnity in favour of the Trustee is, in any event, implied by law, by way of the Trustee's lien, referred to in clause 3 of the deed of appointment. Some trust companies include an express release and indemnity as a matter of course, in respect of which see clause 4 of the deed of appointment.

## 2. Payment of tax and expenses

Any tax, costs and expenses and other liabilities occasioned by the appointment shall be borne by the Beneficiary.

## 3. Trustee's lien

Nothing in this Deed shall prejudice or impair in any way any lien or charge to which the Trustee is entitled in respect of any liabilities for which it may be or become accountable.

## 4. Release and Indemnity of the Trustee 2

## 4.1 Release

[The Beneficiary, so far as he [she] is competent to do so, releases the Trustee entirely in respect of the assets in the Appointed Fund and the income thereof and from all claims, demands, actions, proceedings and accounts of any kind on the part of any person actually or prospectively interested under the Trust in the Trust Fund or the income of the Trust Fund or the trusts of the Trust or an act or thing done or omitted in execution or purported execution of such trusts other than and except only actions arising from any fraud, wilful misconduct or dishonesty on the part of the Trustee.]

## 4.2 Indemnity

[The Beneficiary promises the Trustee to keep it fully and effectually indemnified entirely from and against all existing, future or contingent liabilities, claims, demands, actions, proceedings and accounts of any kind (and any costs and expenses incurred in connection therewith) which may be brought or threatened to be brought by any person against the Trustee in connection with the Trust or any instruments or deeds supplemental thereto including this Deed or in any way relating to the Trust Fund or any act or thing done by the Trustee arising directly or indirectly or omitted in the execution of the trusts of the Trust and such indemnity shall extend to any claims or demands for taxes, duties or other fiscal liabilities payable in any part of the world in respect of the Trust Fund whether or not such claims or demands are legally enforceable against the Trustee in its jurisdiction of residence PROVIDED ALWAYS THAT:

- (1) such indemnity shall not operate to indemnify the Trustee for liabilities it would not have been entitled to an indemnity for under the terms of the Trust;
- (2) the liability of the Beneficiary under this indemnity shall be limited to the value of the Appointed Fund.]

## EXECUTION

IN WITNESS whereof the parties have executed and delivered this Deed of Appointment as a deed on the date stated at the beginning of it.
