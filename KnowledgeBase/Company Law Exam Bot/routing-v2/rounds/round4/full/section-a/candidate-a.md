### MCQ01

**Stem:** AB Ltd has traditional articles reproducing Appendix 1B article 23.1. It has distributable profits and proposes a final cash dividend. Which document route must be used?

**Polarity:** `correct`; the qualifier `must` asks for the mandatory route under the supplied article.

**Answer:** **A** — Appendix 19A board recommendation followed by Appendix 19B members' declaration.

**Why:** Traditional article 23.1 requires the members to declare the dividend, and the amount cannot exceed the directors' recommendation. The course therefore requires the two-organ sequence: board recommendation, then members' declaration.

- **Option A:** supported — it is the two-step traditional route.
- **Option B:** refuted — a board-only dividend is the alternative streamlined article route, not the stated article 23.1 route.
- **Option C:** refuted — the solvency-distribution precedent belongs to the BVI/IBC regime, not these traditional articles.
- **Option D:** refuted — repayment of shareholder debt is not a final dividend.

**Closest two:** A/B — whether the governing articles require members' declaration in addition to the board recommendation, or give the board the alternative sole power.

**Sources used:** `Course-Manual-Module-06-Equity-Capital-and-Distributions.md` §6.1; `Course-Manual-Module-10-Company-Decision-making-Procedures.md` §5.7; `Appendix-1B-Articles-of-Association-based-upon-Table-A-1948-Act-with.md` art.23.1; `Appendix-19A-Directors-resolution-to-recommend-payment-of-a-dividend.md`; `Appendix-19B-Members-resolution-to-declare-and-pay-a-dividend.md`.

**Cross-check:** M10 §5.7 incorporated; it independently confirms the traditional two-organ procedure. The board-only, solvency, and loan routes were discarded as wrong article/regime/stage.

**Confidence:** high, 10/10 — exact article and both operative precedents determine the letter.
