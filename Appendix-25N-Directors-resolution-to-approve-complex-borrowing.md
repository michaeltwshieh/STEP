## Appendix 25N: Director's resolution to approve complex borrowing transaction

## AB LIMITED (Company No. [number])

## WRITTEN RESOLUTIONS OF SOLE DIRECTOR OF AB LIMITED

Pursuant to art.[x] of the Company's Articles of Association, we the undersigned sole director of the Company for the time being:

## DEFINE :

'Facility Letter A' means a copy of a letter dated [date] from Lender A to the Company offering overdraft and other facilities totalling $10 million.

'Facility Letter B' means a copy of a letter dated [date] from Lender B to the Company offering a loan facility of $5 million and other facilities to the Company.

'Floating Charge A' means a floating charge [Debenture] intended to be made between the Company and Lender A to give to Lender A a floating charge over all the Company's undertaking and movable assets including goodwill and uncalled capital as security for all moneys and liabilities from time to time owing or incurred by the Company to Lender A.

'Fixed Legal Charge B' means a first legal charge [mortgage] intended to be made between the Company and Lender B to give to Lender B a first fixed legal charge over its freehold property located and known as [description] as security for all moneys and liabilities from time to time owing or incurred by the Company to Lender B.

'Priority Deed' means a deed of priorities between Lender A, Lender B and the Company. 'Security Documents' means the above defined Facility Letter A, Facility Letter B, Floating Charge A, Fixed Legal Charge B and Priority Deed.

## NOTE :

- (A) THAT Lender A had agreed to make available and continue overdraft and other facilities to the Company as set out or referred to in the Facility Letter A and Lender B had agreed to make available to the Company the facilities as set out or referred to in the Facility Letter B.
- (B) THAT the legal effect of Floating Charge A will be to charge the whole of the Company's undertaking and movable assets with the liabilities to the Lender A.
- (C) THAT the legal effect of Fixed Legal Charge B will be to charge by way of fixed mortgage the Company's freehold property located and known as [description] to Lender B.

- (D) THAT the director has fully and carefully considered the terms of the Security Documents, the nature and scale of the liabilities undertaken pursuant to them and the commercial and financial consequences of executing them so far as they affect the Company, its financial position and its future trading and financial requirements.
- (E) THAT there are no limits on the power of the Company or its director to borrow money or create security and the execution of the Security Documents are within the powers conferred upon the board of the Company.
- (F) THAT the transactions effected by the Security Documents are in the best interests of the Company and entered into in good faith and for the purpose of carrying on the business of the Company and there are reasonable grounds for believing that the transactions will be for the benefit of the Company.

## RESOLVE :

1. That the Security Documents be and are hereby approved.
2. That the duplicate of each of the Facility Letters be signed by the director on behalf of the Company and delivered to Lender A and Lender B with certified copies of these resolutions and the Company's service provider be and is hereby authorised to pay the respective arrangement fees.
3. That each of the Floating Charge A, the Fixed Legal Charge B, and the Priority Deed be executed as a deed by the director, but with the intention that they will not be delivered until they are dated. [… by the director and registered agent affixing the Company's common seal and that the affixing of the seal be witnessed by the director and registered agent of the Company.
4. That the director be and is hereby authorised to agree amendments to the Security Document and to agree, negotiate and execute whether under hand or as a deed and deliver any other documents which are required in connection with the proposed transactions.

DATED

SIGNED
