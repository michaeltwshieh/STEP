## Deed of Settlement: Discretionary Trust with

## Appendix 1: Alternative Reserved Powers

## THIS DEED OF SETTLEMENT is made [date] BETWEEN

1. [Name] of [address] (the 'Settlor'), and
2. [Name] Trust Company Ltd., a company incorporated under the laws of [jurisdiction] whose registered office is situated at [address] (the 'Trustees')

## RECITALS

- (A) The Settlor wishes to make this Settlement in order to provide for [his/her family and friends].
- (B) The Settlor has transferred or delivered to the Trustees or otherwise placed under their control the Trust Fund specified in clause 1.1 hereof to be held on trust upon the terms hereinafter set out.
- (C) The Settlor shall transfer all the issued shares in [Name of Underlying Company] to the Trustees to be accepted by them as an addition to the Trust Fund. Further money, investments or other property may be paid or transferred to the Trustees by way of further additions to be held upon the trusts hereof.
- (D) It is intended that this Settlement shall be known as the [Name] Trust.

## OPERATIVE PROVISIONS

## NOW THIS DEED WITNESSES

## 1. Definitions and construction

In this Deed where the context admits, the following definitions apply.

- 1.1 The 'Trust Fund' means:
- (a) the initial trust fund is the nominal sum of US$1.00; and
- (b) the shares of [Name of Underlying Company] that shall be issued to the Trustees as an addition; and
- (c) all money, investments or other property transferred to the Trustees (or [the Underlying Company]) by the Settlor or any other person as further additions; and all accumulations (if any) of income added to the Trust Fund; and the money, investments and property from time to time representing the above.
- 1.2 The 'Trust Period' means the period ending on last day of the period of [number of years not exceeding the statutory perpetuity period, if any] from the date of this Deed, which period shall be the applicable perpetuity period. [If the Governing Law does not prescribe a statutory maximum period then the Trust Period shall continue until such day (if any) as the Trustees may [with the written consent of the Settlor [Protector]] may, in their discretion, declare by deed.]

- 1.3 The 'Beneficiaries' means:
- (a) the Settlor's children and remoter issue;
- (b) the spouses, widows and widowers (but not such widows or widowers who have remarried) of the Settlor's children and remoter issue;
- (c) such other objects or persons as are added under clause 2 of this Deed.
- 1.4 The 'Protector' means [name] or such other persons as shall, for the time being, be the protector of this Trust.
- 1.5 ['Excluded Person' means:
- (a) the Settlor
- (b) any person who is an Excluded Person under clause 3].
- 1.6 The 'Alternate Trustees' means such persons as may be appointed under clause 11.
- 1.7 The expression 'the Trustees' shall, where the context admits, include the trustees for the time being of this Trust.
- 1.8 The 'Governing law' means the laws of the jurisdiction governing this Trust stipulated in clause 16 hereof.

## 2. Power to add Beneficiaries

- 2.1 The Settlor [Protector] may, [or the Trustees may (with the written consent of the Protector)] at any time during the Trust Period, add to the Beneficiaries such objects or persons or classes of objects or persons as the Settlor [Protector] [Trustees] shall determine.
- 2.2 Any such addition shall be made by deed:
- (a) naming or describing the objects or persons or classes of objects or persons to be added; and
- (b) specifying the date or event, not being earlier than the date of execution of the deed but before the end of the Trust Period, on the happening of which the addition shall take effect.
- (c) [This power shall not be exercised so as to add an Excluded Person to the Beneficiaries.]

## 3. Power of exclusion

- 3.1 The Settlor [Protector] may [the Trustees may (with the written consent of the Protector)], at any time during the Trust Period, declare by deed that the objects or persons named or specified, whether or not ascertained, in such deed who are, would or might, but for this clause, be or become Beneficiaries, shall, in relation to the whole or any part of the Trust Fund, be Excluded Persons.
- 3.2 The power conferred by sub-clause 3.1 shall not be capable of being exercised so as to derogate from any interest to which any of the Beneficiaries has previously become indefeasibly entitled, whether in possession or in reversion or otherwise.
- 3.3 No discretion or power conferred on the Settlor [Protector] or Trustees or any other person by this Deed or by law shall be exercised, and no provision of this Deed shall operate directly or indirectly, so as to cause or permit any

part of the capital or income of the Trust Fund to become in any way payable to or applicable for the benefit of any Excluded Person.

## 4. [Trust for sale]

[The Trustees shall hold the Trust Fund upon trust (as to investments or property other than money) in their absolute discretion to sell, call in or convert into money such investments or property but with power to postpone such sale, calling in or conversion and to permit the same to remain as invested.]

## 5. [Power to receive additional property]

[The Trustees may, at any time during the Trust Period, accept additional money, investments or other property, of whatever nature and wherever situate, transferred to them by the Settlor or any other person. Such additional money, investments or other property shall, subject to any contrary direction, be held upon the trusts and with and subject to the powers and provisions of this Deed.]

## 6. Power of disposition of capital and income

The Trustees shall hold the capital and income of the Trust Fund upon the following trusts:

## 6.1 Trust Income

- 6.1.1 The Trustees may accumulate the whole or any part of the income generated by the Trust Fund during the Trust Period, and that income shall be added to the Trust Fund.
- 6.1.2 The Trustees may pay or apply the income generated by the Trust Fund, or the remainder not accumulated under the provisions of clause 6.1.1 above, for or for the benefit of such of the Beneficiaries, at such ages or times, in such shares, upon such trusts (which may include discretionary or protective powers or trusts) and in such manner generally as the Trustees [Settlor] may in their [his/her] discretion think fit.
- 6.1.3 [The exercise of the dispositive powers conferred by sub-clause 6.1.2 shall be exercisable only with the written consent of the Settlor [Protector].

## Alternatively

[The exercise of the dispositive powers conferred by sub-clause 6.1.2 shall be exercised by the Trustees in accordance with the directions, if any, given in writing from time to time by the Settlor [Protector] [provided that the prior written consent of the Protector is also obtained]].

## 6.2 Trust Capital

## Power of appointment

- 6.2.1 The Trustees may pay or apply the whole or any part of the Trust Fund, or may appoint and declare by deed that they hold the whole or any part thereof, on behalf or for the benefit of, all or such one or more of the Beneficiaries in such shares and upon such trusts (including discretionary or protective trusts or powers) and in such manner generally as the Trustees may, in their absolute discretion, think fit.

## Power of re-settlement

- 6.2.2   The Trustees may declare by deed that they hold the whole or any part of the Trust Fund on trust to transfer it to the trustees of another trust, wherever and whenever established, to hold on the terms of that trust, freed and released from the terms of the Trust, provided that any one or more persons who may benefit is a Beneficiary.

## Exercise of powers of appointment and re-settlement

- 6.2.3 [The exercise of the dispositive powers of appointment and re-settlement of capital conferred by sub-clauses 6.2.1 and 6.2.2 shall be exercisable only with the written consent of the Settlor [Protector].

## Alternatively

[The exercise of the dispositive powers of appointment and re-settlement of capital of the Trust Fund conferred by sub-clauses 6.2.1 and 6.2.2 shall be exercised by the Trustees in accordance with the directions, if any, given in writing from time to time by the Settlor [Protector] [provided that the prior written consent of the Protector is also obtained]].

## 6.3 Ultimate default trusts

Subject as above and if and so far as not wholly disposed of for any reason whatever by the above provisions, the capital and income of the Trust Fund shall be held upon trust for [charity] absolutely.

## 7. Administrative powers

The Trustees shall, in addition to all common law and statutory powers, have the powers [and immunities] set out in the Schedule of this Deed.

## 8. Protection of the Trustees

## 8.1 Indemnity

Any liability incurred by or imposed by law on the Trustees by reason of any transaction, act or omission in the administration of the trusts hereof shall be met out of the Trust Fund other than arising out of the fraud, wilful misconduct, or the bad faith of the Trustees.

## 8.2 Exoneration

The Trustees shall not be liable for any loss suffered by the Trust Fund arising by reason of:

- (1) any mistake or omission made in good faith by such trustee, or by any of the Trustees; or any other matter or thing, except fraud, wilful default or dishonesty of such trustee, and, the negligence or fraud of any agent employed by such trustee, in good faith;
- (2) the Trustees acting in compliance with or as the result of the exercise by the Protector [Settlor] of his [her] power in paragraph [1.5 of the Second Schedule, namely a direction to invest].

## 9. Trustee [and Protector] charging clause

- 9.1 The Trustees, carrying on trust business as a trust company licensed to undertake trust business, shall be entitled, in addition to reimbursement of its proper expenses, to remuneration for its services in accordance with its published terms and conditions for trust business in force from time to time [or in accordance with such terms and conditions as may from time to time be agreed between such trustee and the Protector].
- 9.2 The Protector [who is an attorney or other person engaged in a profession or business or in a personal capacity], shall be entitled to charge, and be paid out of the Trust Fund, all normal professional or other charges for business done, services rendered or time spent by him [her] personally in the administration of these trusts, including acts which a protector not engaged in any profession or business could have done personally.
- 9.3 Any Alternate Trustee shall be entitled, in addition to reimbursement of proper expenses, to such remuneration for services as may from time to time be agreed between such Alternate Trustee and the Protector.
- 9.4 The Trustees may act and be remunerated as a director or other officer, or as agent or adviser of any company that holds assets for and on behalf of the Trustees or is in any way connected with the Trust Fund and shall not be liable to account for any remuneration, fees or profits received by the Trustee in any such capacity.
- 9.5 The Trustees (or entities associated with the Trustees) may act as banker, broker or other agent and may retain any fees, brokerage or other commission received by them for any transaction carried out for the Trustees for which they are in the normal course of business allowed remuneration of commission.

## 10. Power of removal and appointment of Trustees

- 10.1 The Protector [Settlor] may by deed [written notice] served upon the Trustees appoint new or additional trustees of this trust.
- 10.2 If the Protector [Settlor] is unable, unwilling or unfit to act, or if there shall be no Protector, the power of appointing new trustees shall be vested in the Trustees.
- 10.3 The Protector [Settlor] shall also have power, exercisable by deed [written notice], to remove the Trustees as trustee of this Trust.

## 11. Appointment of Alternate Trustees

- 11.1 During the Trust Period, the Protector may, at any time, by deed delivered to the Trustees, appoint an Alternate Trustee.
- 11.2 No person may be appointed to be an Alternate Trustee if he or she is resident in a jurisdiction in which any of the Trustees are, at the date of such appointment, also resident.

## 12. Automatic retirement of Trustees

- 12.1 On the happening of any of the events specified in clause 13 (the 'Emergency Event') any Trustee resident in the jurisdiction in which the Emergency Event occurs shall automatically cease to be a trustee and is referred to below as the 'Retiring Trustee' .

- 12.2 Upon the Retiring Trustee ceasing to be trustee of this Trust upon the occurrence of an Emergency Event, the Alternate Trustee shall become the new Trustee (the 'New Trustee') in place of the Retiring Trustee.
- 12.3 The Retiring Trustee shall be divested of title to all the assets comprised in the Trust Fund and those assets shall vest automatically in the New Trustee. [The Trustees irrevocably grant power of attorney to the New Trustees to transfer the Trust Fund to themselves as New Trustee on behalf of the Retiring Trustee.]
- 12.4 If, on the happening of the Emergency Event, the governing law of this Trust is that of the jurisdiction where the Emergency Event occurs or such jurisdiction is the forum for the administration of this Trust, then, on the happening of the Emergency Event, the governing law of this Trust and the forum for the administration of this Trust shall, notwithstanding any other provision of this Trust, automatically become the jurisdiction where the Alternate Trustee is resident.

## 13. Events precipitating the automatic retirement of the Trustee

The events in which the provisions of clause 13 shall take effect are:

- 13.1 an attack upon, or the occupation of, the territory in question by hostile military forces;
- 13.2 the occurrence in that territory of civil war or of violent civil disturbances which cause, or appear likely to cause, a serious and prolonged interference with the ability of the trustee to function as such;
- 13.3 the acquisition by the government, or any other public authority, of that territory of a controlling interest in the trustee; or
- 13.4 the enactment of any law, or the taking of any action by the government, or any other public authority, of that territory, the effect of which is (or would be if the trustee in question had sole control of the assets comprised in the Trust Fund)
- (a) to expropriate or confiscate any assets comprised in, or to require the making of any substantial payment out of, the Trust Fund, other than a payment required in the ordinary course of taxation (not being of a penal or confiscatory nature) ;
- (b) to determine or restrict substantially the power of the trustee to transfer assets comprising income or capital of the Trust Fund out of that territory for the purpose of investment or distribution; or
- (c) to interfere materially in the way in which the Trustees administer the Trust Fund or exercise the power and discretions vested in them under this Trust.

## 14. Successor Protectors

- 14.1 The Protector shall cease to be the Protector on death (if an individual), or on dissolution (if a corporation), or, in either case, on becoming unable or unfit to act or making a valid appointment under sub-clause 14.2.
- 14.2 The Protector may, by deed, appoint any other person to be protector of this Trust and, if the Trustees have been given written notice of the appointment and the person appointed consents in writing, such person shall immediately become the Protector.

- 14.3 The Protector may, by deed during his lifetime, nominate a person to become the Protector upon the person making the nomination dying or otherwise ceasing to be the Protector. Upon such cessation, if the nomination remains unrevoked, the Trustees have been given written notice of the nomination and the person nominated consents in writing, such person shall immediately become the Protector.
- 14.4 If, notwithstanding the provisions of sub-clause 14.1. to 14.3. there shall at any time be no Protector of this Trust, the [Settlor] Trustees may, by deed, irrevocably appoint any person, not being one of the Trustees, to be the Protector.

## 15. Protection of the Protector

The Protector shall not be liable for any loss to the Trust Fund arising in consequence of the failure, depreciation or loss of any investment, or any mistake, omission or any other matter or thing, except caused by the wilful default, fraud or bad faith on the part of the Protector.

## 16. Governing law, forum and place of administration

- 16.1 The governing law of this trust shall be that of [jurisdiction].
- 16.2 The courts of [jurisdiction] shall be the exclusive forum for the adjudication of any issues in dispute and administration of these trusts.
- 16.3 Notwithstanding the provisions of sub-clauses 16.1 and 16.2, the Trustees may at any time, [with the written consent of the Protector], declare in writing that, from the date of such declaration, the governing law of this Trust shall be that of any specified jurisdiction. [No exercise of this power shall be effective unless the law of the jurisdiction specified is one under which this Trust remains irrevocable and all or substantially all of the trusts, powers and provisions contained in this Deed remain enforceable and capable of being exercised and so taking effect.]

## 17. Miscellaneous Powers of Protector

## 17.1 Take counsel's opinion

The Protector may obtain the opinion of legal counsel or tax advisers locally or in any other jurisdiction of the world where necessary or appropriate concerning any matter relating to the Trust or the tax or fiscal position of the Trustees, the Beneficiaries, or on any matter relating to this Trust, and the cost thereof shall be met out of the Trust Fund.

## 17.2 Appoint auditors

The Trustees shall, upon receipt of a direction in writing by the Protector appoint independent auditors to examine and/or audit the trust accounts maintained by the Trustees relating to the affairs of the Trust in such manner as may be determined from time to time by the Protector, and the cost of such audit shall be met out of the capital or income of the Trust Fund.

## 17.3 Deliver trust accounts and other information

The Trustees shall, upon receipt of an instruction in writing, deliver copies of the Trust accounts and such other information relating to the administration of the Trust and management of the Trust Fund as the Protector may reasonably require to enable the Protector:

- (a) to undertake regular reviews of the trust administration
- (b) to approve the trust accounts on behalf of the Beneficiaries
- (c) to settle questions of construction or other disputes concerning the administration of the Trust

## 17.4 Non-disclosure

Subject to an order of any competent court, the Trustees shall not disclose to any person, including one or all of the Beneficiaries, any document or other matter relating to this Trust unless the Protector gives his [her] prior consent in writing.

## THE SCHEDULE

## The Trustee's powers and immunities

## 1. Investment of the Trust Fund

## Power to invest

- 1.1 [Subject to the provision of sub-clause 1.4. below] any money to be invested may be invested in or upon any investment of whatever nature [including the purchase or acquisition (either alone or jointly with other persons) in any real or personal property, including investments in any currency in any part of the world, investments in shares, stock, funds, securities, bonds, notes, debentures, time deposits, land, buildings, chattels or other property of whatever nature and wherever situate, and whether involving liabilities or producing income or not, or in making such loans, with or without security], as the Trustees think fit.

## Modification of Trustee's duties

- 1.2 The Trustees shall not be under a duty or required to invest the Trust Fund in a prudent manner, nor to invest in low-risk securities, nor to diversify the investments of the Trust Fund and may invest in high-risk, speculative, concentrated or hazardous investments.
- 1.3 [The Trustees shall not be liable for loss arising from their investment of the Trust Fund:
- (a) in a non-prudent, high-risk, speculative manner, or
- (b) in a single business, company or firm or in one asset or type of asset.
- 1.4 The Trustees shall not be under a duty to invest in assets that produce an income and may invest in non-income producing investments or securities that do not produce an income.
- 1.5 [The exercise of the power of investment conferred by sub-clause 1.1. above shall be exercisable only with the written consent of the Settlor [Protector].

## Alternatively,

[The exercise of the power of investment conferred by sub-clause 1.1. above shall be exercised by the Trustees in accordance with the directions, if any, given in writing from time to time by the Settlor [Protector].

## 2. Power to lend and give guarantees

- 2.1 The Trustees may lend money or assets comprised in the Trust Fund to any Beneficiary, [and/or Settlor] on such terms (whether or not including provision for the payment of interest) as the Trustees think fit.
- 2.2 The Trustees may guarantee the payment of money and the performance of obligations by any Beneficiary [or by any company in which the Trust Fund is invested] and may charge all or any part of the Trust Fund in support of such guarantees.

## 3. Powers in relation to real property and chattels

## 3.1 Land

The powers conferred by this sub-clause shall be exercisable where the Trust Fund for the time being includes any real or immovable property (in this clause referred to as the 'land').

- 3.1.1 The Trustees may lease all or any part of the land for any purpose, and for any term, in consideration of a rent (whether fixed or variable), or premium, and may accept (with or without consideration) the surrender of any lease of all or any part of the land.
- 3.1.2 The Trustees may in executing any trust or power of sale:
- (a) sell or lease all or any part of the land, whether the division is horizontal or vertical;
- (b) sell, lease or reserve any easement, right or privilege over all or any part of  the land;
- (c) sell, lease, or reserve any timber, mines or minerals on, in or under all or any part of the land;
- (d) impose, and make binding for the benefit of all or any part of the land sold or leased, any restrictions as to user affecting any part of the land retained;
- (e) enter into any contract or grant any option for the sale of all or any part of the land.
- 3.1.3 The Trustees shall not be bound to see, nor be liable or accountable for omitting or neglecting to see, to the repair of any building or other structure on the land, or to the payment of any outgoings, or otherwise as to the maintenance of the land, or any building or other structure on the land, but may maintain and repair the same in such manner and to such extent as they think fit.

## 3.2 Chattels

The powers conferred by this sub-clause shall be exercisable where the Trust Fund for the time being includes any chattels (in this clause referred to as the 'chattels').

- 3.2.1 The Trustees may sell, lease, hire, deposit, store or otherwise deal with the chattels upon such terms as they think fit.
- 3.2.2 The Trustees shall not be bound to see, nor be liable or accountable for omitting or neglecting to see, to the safe custody, maintenance or repair of the chattels, but may see to such safe custody, maintenance and repair in such manner and to such extent as they think fit.

## 4. Power to trade, operate a business

- 4.1 The Trustees may trade or operate a business, or take part in any venture in the nature of trade, either alone or jointly with any other person, and whether or not by way of partnership (limited or general).
- 4.2 The Trustees shall be entitled to be fully indemnified out of the Trust Fund against all personal liability to which they may become in any manner subject in connection with any such trade or venture.

## 5. Power to insure property

- 5.1 The Trustees may insure any property comprised in the Trust Fund against any loss or damage from any risk for any amount, and may pay the premiums out of the Trust Fund or the income as may be appropriate.
- 5.2 The Trustees shall not be bound to see to such insurance, nor be liable or accountable for omitting or neglecting to see to it, or for its adequacy.

## 6. Power in relation to companies

## 6.1 Power to promote companies

The Trustees may (without prejudice to the generality of their powers of investment) promote or incorporate any company in any part of the world:

- (a) to establish and carrying on by such company a business of any kind which the Trustees are for the time being authorised to carry on themselves and the acquisition of any of the assets comprised in the Trust Fund which may be required for the purposes of such business;
- (b) to acquire all or any of the assets comprised in the Trust Fund to be held as investments of the company.

## 6.2 Power to Vote

The Trustees may exercise the voting rights attached to shares of companies held in the Trust Fund, including to waive the appointment of auditors, and presentation of audited accounts to shareholders.

## 6.3 Power to enter into any compromise/arrangement

The Trustees may enter into any compromise or arrangement or winding up in relation to any company in which the Trust Fund is invested.

## 6.4 No requirement to interfere in the management of companies

The Trustees are under no duty to enquire into, to supervise, be involved or interfere in the management or conduct of the business of any company in which the Trust Fund is invested, holds shares or is otherwise involved and may leave the conduct of the business and affairs of the company wholly to the board [unless they have knowledge of circumstances that call for enquiry.]

## 7. Power to delegate and appoint agents

## 7.1 Power to delegate generally

The Trustees [and the Protector] may, instead of acting personally, employ and pay at the expense of the Trust Fund any agents in any part of the world, [whether accountants, attorneys, brokers, bankers, trust companies or other agents] to transact any business in connection with this trust, including the receipt and payment of money, the execution of documents and the holding of any securities, property or documents, without being responsible for the fraud, dishonesty or negligence of each agent provided employed in good faith.

## 7.2 Power to employ nominees

The Trustees may invest or hold, or allow to remain in the name of any other person as nominee of the Trustees, any assets comprised in the Trust Fund, on such terms and conditions as the Trustees think fit.

## 7.3 Power to appoint investment advisers and delegate management of investments.

- 7.3.1 The Trustees may engage the services of any entity, person or partnership (the 'Investment Manager') to advise upon or to manage the investments comprised in the whole or such part of the Trust Fund within the limits and for the period stipulated by the Trustees. The Trustees may settle the terms and conditions for the engagement of, and any delegation to, the Investment Manager including, if they think fit, terms and conditions as to remuneration and reimbursement of the Investment Manager's expenses at the expense of the Trust Fund.
- 7.3.2 The Trustees shall incur no liability for following the advice of the Investment Manager, nor shall the Trustee be liable for loss arising as a result of the delegation to the Investment Manager of the discretion to manage the investment of the Trust Fund.

## 8. Powers to sue and/or compromise claims

- 8.1 The Trustees may bring or defend any lawsuits, actions or other legal proceedings affecting the Trustees or the Trust Fund.
- 8.2 The Trustees may compromise any matters of difference, compound any debt or claim and/or adjust any disputes in relation to debts or claims made by or against them as the Trustees think fit, in each case upon the evidence that the Trustees shall, in good faith,  deem proper without being responsible for any loss occasioned thereby.

## 9. Power to appropriate or distribute in specie

The Trustees may make any distribution of the Trust Fund pursuant to the Trusts of this Deed in cash or in kind, or partly in cash and in kind, and may for this purpose, appropriate and/or apportion the Trust Fund in any manner that the Trustees think fit.
