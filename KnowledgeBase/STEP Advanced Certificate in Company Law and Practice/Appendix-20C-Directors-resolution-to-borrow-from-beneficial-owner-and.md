## Appendix 20C:

## Director's resolution to borrow from beneficial owner and execute pro note

## AB Limited (Company [number])

## WRITTEN RESOLUTIONS OF THE SOLE DIRECTOR OF AB LIMITED (the 'Company')

Pursuant to art.[x] of the Company's Articles of Association, the undersigned sole director of the Company for the time being:

## HEREBY NOTES:

- (A) THAT [name of beneficial owner] has agreed to lend the Company of the sum of $[1 million] on an unsecured basis, repayable on demand without interest.
- (B) THAT the borrowing would promote the success of the Company and be in its best interests
- (C) THAT a copy of a promissory note has been produced and is attached marked 'AB1' (the 'Promissory Note')

## HEREBY RESOLVES:

1. THAT that the terms and conditions of the borrowing by the Company from [name of beneficial owner] be and are hereby approved.
2. THAT the sole director be and is hereby authorised to execute the Promissory Note on behalf of the Company in the presence of a witness.
