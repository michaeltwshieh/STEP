# Appendix 31: Foundation Charter of the AB Foundation

## Appendix 31: Foundation Charter of the AB Foundation

## Foundation No. 12345N

## Article 1: Name

The undersigned [ name ] (the ' Founder' ) in the capacity as founder, hereby registers and constitutes in accordance with the governing foundation legislation a private foundation by the name of the AB Foundation (hereinafter called 'the Foundation ') as a legal entity in its own right.

## Article 2: Object or purposes

The purposes of the Foundation is to protect, preserve, invest, manage and, where reasonable, enhance its assets to promote the long-term interests of the Founder's family, and to dispose of such assets to and for the benefit of the Beneficiaries in accordance with the dispositive provisions set out in the Regulations of the Foundation established pursuant to Article 7.

## Article 3: Initial Endowment

- 3.1 The initial Endowment of the Foundation is US$10,000 (Ten thousand United States dollars).
- 3.2 The initial Endowment may at any time be increased by additional endowments made by the Founder, or any other third party.

## Article 4: the Foundation Council

- 4.1 The Foundation Council is responsible for the management and administration of the Foundation and is authorised to exercise all the powers of the Foundation.
- 4.2 The initial Foundation Council shall comprise of:
- (i) Alpha Limited of PO Box 123, [ address ], and
- (ii) Beta Limited of PO Box 1234, [ address ].
- 4.3 The Founder may for any cause whatsoever, during his lifetime, remove any one or more members of the Foundation Council and appoint a replacement. After the death of the Founder or, in the event of his incapacity, the Supervising Body shall have the power to remove and appoint new Foundation Council members.
- 4.4 The tenure of office of members of the Foundation Council is unlimited.

## Article 5: Duration

- 5.1 The duration of the Foundation is unlimited.
- 5.2 The Foundation may however be dissolved voluntarily by the unanimous resolution of the Foundation Council subject to the further terms of the Regulations.

## Article 6: Beneficiaries

The Founder at the time of creating the Foundation, or subsequently the Foundation Council, may create a private document known as the Regulations which may designate the Beneficiaries, set out the dispositive provisions and confer dispositive powers and/or impose dispositive duties upon the Foundation Council.

## Article 7: Regulations

The Founder at the time of creating the Foundation, or subsequently the Foundation Council, is authorised to issue Regulations to establish, inter alia, administrative and/or dispositive provisions.

## Article 8: Supervisory Body

- 8.1 The Regulations may appoint a Supervisory Body, which may be a natural individual or a corporate entity.
- 8.2 In addition to its powers and duties set out in the Regulations (and relevant foundation legislation enacted as part of the governing law of the state of registration of the Foundation), the Supervisory Body shall ensure the Foundation Council complies with the objects and performs the purposes of the Foundation and shall safeguard the rights and interests of the Foundation.

## Article 9: Governing Law

- 9.1 The governing law the Foundation shall be that of [ identified jurisdiction ].
- 9.2 The Foundation Council may at any time in its discretion transfer the domicile of the Foundation to the jurisdiction of another country.

## Regulations of the AB Foundation

## Foundation No. 12345N

In accordance with the Charter of the AB Foundation, registered as Foundation Number 12345N the Registrar of Foundations of [ identified jurisdiction ], the Foundation Council hereby adopts the following Regulations for the Foundation.

## REGULATION 1: The Council

- 1.1 The Foundation Council is authorised to exercise the powers of the Foundation, and in so doing shall act only within the authority of a valid resolution of the Foundation Council.
- 1.2 The Foundation Council shall have an unrestricted power to borrow on behalf of the Foundation and to charge the assets of the Foundation assets as collateral for such borrowing.
- 1.3 The Foundation may, by power of attorney or otherwise, appoint agents to act on behalf of the Foundation on such terms that the Council thinks fit.
- 1.4 If the Foundation Council comprises of more than one member it may appoint one of its members as an elected president, and another member as secretary and any other officer that the Foundations Council considers appropriate.
- 1.5 The Foundation Council shall meet upon the invitation of the President (but if no President is elected upon the invitation of any member of the Foundation Council) at such place and time as is reasonably stipulated in a notice of such meeting
- 1.6 Resolutions are valid when all members have been duly summoned and when the majority of them are present.
- 1.7 A resolution of the Foundation Council is passed by a simple majority of the members present. In the event of parity of votes, the President has a deciding vote. Should the Foundation Council comprise of two members, a resolution requires unanimity. Should the Foundation Council comprise only one member, such member may alone pass a written resolution to make decisions.
- 1.8 Resolutions of the Foundation Council shall be recorded in writing and minutes shall be signed by all members present.
- 1.9 Resolutions may also be passed by means of a circular written resolution signed by all members of the Foundation Council as, shall have the same legal effect of a resolution passed at a duly held meeting of the Foundation Council.
- 1.10 The Foundation Council is entitled by resolution to delegate its powers and its responsibility for the management and administration of the Foundation to one or more members of the Foundation Council or to a third party.
- 1.11 The signature of any one member of the Foundation Council, provided that such person or entity is properly authorised by resolution, is required in order for any contract or transaction to be binding upon the Foundation.

## REGULATION 2: Financial affairs

- 2.1 The Foundation Council shall keep such books and records that accurately record all receipts and expenditure, revenues of, and distributions from, the assets of the Foundation.
- 2.2 The Foundation Council may prepare annual financial statements recording the assets and liabilities, profits and losses, of the Foundation in such form and according to which accounting standards they deem appropriate and shall render such accounts to the Founder and to, or at the direction of, the Supervisory Body.

## REGULATION 3: Foundation Seal

- 3.1 If the Foundation Council considers it appropriate, the Council may adopt and have its own Foundation Seal.
- 3.2 The Foundation Seal shall be used as directed by resolution of the Foundation Council.

## REGULATION 4: Investment

- 4.1 The Foundation may, by resolution of the Foundation Council, invest in any property whatsoever and in any currency in any part of the world, including real estate, personal moveable chattels, stocks, shares and other securities as they think fit.
- 4.2 The Foundation Council may by resolution engage the services of an investment adviser to advise upon the investment of the Foundation's assets. or an investment manager to manage the assets of the Foundation on a discretionary basis (the ' Investment Adviser' ).
- 4.3 If the Foundation Council by unanimous resolution so decides, it may remove and replace the Investment Adviser with such other Investment Adviser as it considers appropriate in the circumstances.
- 4.4 The members of the Foundation Council shall not be liable for losses suffered by the Foundation arising from an investment made upon the advice or management of the Foundation's assets by an Investment Adviser who is competent and qualified to act as such and has been engaged in good faith PROVIDED that such losses are  not caused by the fraud, dishonesty or wilful default of the members of the Foundation Council in the performance of their duties.

## REGULATION 5: the Supervisory Body

- 5.1 The initial Supervisory Body shall be Delta (Nominees) Limited of PO Box 4321 [ address ].
- 5.2 The Founder may during his lifetime, remove and replace such Supervisory Body in his discretion. After the death of the Founder, the Foundation Council has power to remove and replace the Supervisory Body provided they shall obtain the prior approval of a majority of the Beneficiaries (as defined below) who are alive and sui juris .
- 5.3 The Supervisory Body may require the Foundation Council to account for their management of the assets and revenues of the Foundation and to produce such accounts, and supporting documents such as receipts, vouchers and other recorded data, that the Supervisory Body may specify and reasonably require in order to perform appropriate supervision and oversight.

- 5.4 The Supervisory Body may at any time appoint one or more persons as auditor(s) to audit the books and records of the Foundation upon such terms as it thinks fit in the circumstances.
- 5.5 The Supervising Body shall establish and periodically review the remuneration of the members of the Foundation Council.

## REGULATION 6: Amendment of the Regulations

- 6.1 During the lifetime of the Founder, the Foundation Council may, with the approval of the Fonder, amend these Regulations.
- 6.1 After the death of the Founder, these Regulations shall be irrevocable and not capable of being amended save for any administrative provisions which may only be amended by the Foundation Council with the unanimous approval of all the Supervisory Body.

## REGULATION 7: Dissolution

- 7.1 The Foundation has an unrestricted duration but may, however, be dissolved voluntarily by the Supervisory Body or by the unanimous resolution of the Foundation Council.
- 7.2 When resolving to liquidate the Foundation, the Supervisory Body or the Foundation Council, as the case may be, are authorised to appoint a liquidator when it is deemed necessary to do so.
- 7.3 In dissolving the Foundation, after the payment of any and all debts and obligations of the Foundation, the liquidator shall distribute the surplus assets to the Beneficiaries in accordance with the provisions of the Regulation 8.

## REGULATION 8: the Dispositive Provisions

- 8.1 In this Regulation, the following terms are defined as follows:
- (i) 'The Primary Beneficiary' is the Founder;
- (ii) 'The Secondary Beneficiary' is the spouse of the Founder;
- (iii) 'The Successive Beneficiaries' are the legitimate children and remoter issue of the Founder;
- (iv) The 'Ultimate Beneficiary' means the Red Cross;
- (v) The 'Beneficiaries' means those individual persons identified in sub-clause (i) through (iii) above.

## Primary Beneficiary

- 8.2 During his lifetime, the Primary Beneficiary shall be solely entitled to the enjoyment of and distributions from the Foundation's assets and its income in such amount and at such time as the Foundation Council may from time to time by resolution consider appropriate.

## Secondary Beneficiary

- 8.3 Upon the death of the Primary Beneficiary, the Secondary Beneficiary, if any, shall during her lifetime be entitled to the enjoyment of and distributions from the Foundation's assets and its income in such amount and at such time as the Foundation Council may from time to time by resolution consider appropriate.

## Successive Beneficiaries

- 8.4 Upon the death of the Secondary Beneficiary (or if such Secondary Beneficiary pre-deceases or fails to survive the Primary Beneficiary for more than 30 days, then upon the death of the Primary Beneficiary) the Foundation Council may distribute all or any such part of the Foundation's assets and its income to or for the benefit of such one or more of the Successive Beneficiaries at such time and in such shares as the Foundation Council think fit.

## Power to add to, or exclude from, the class of Successive Beneficiaries

- 8.5 The Foundation Council may, with the consent of the Founder during his lifetime, or with the consent of the Supervisory Body after his death, in their discretion by written declaration add persons or other entities to the class of Successive Beneficiaries.
- 8.6 The Foundation Council may, with the consent of the Founder during his lifetime, or with the consent of the Supervisory Body after his death, in their discretion by declaration exclude persons or other entities to the class of Successive Beneficiaries.

## Default Beneficiary

- 8.7 Should there be no Beneficiaries in existence, or should it not be possible to ascertain any such Beneficiaries, the remaining net assets of the Foundation are to be endowed to the Ultimate Beneficiary.
