## Marks lost, classified

1. **0.25 - omitted:** no express statement of the 25% beneficial-ownership registration threshold. Mr X is treated correctly as the beneficial owner and the BVI record is required, so this is a missing legal detail rather than a wrong conclusion.
2. **0.25 - insufficiently applied:** the answer does not expressly state the complete KAP corporate-recording step for advisers and the bank account: sole-director written resolution plus retention in the company's minute book.

No marks should be lost for the following:

- **The 50,000 no-par-value share structure.** It differs from the KAP's illustrative `$50,000 divided into 50,000 shares of $1 each`, but is a substantively equivalent and more precise application of the course's modern BVI BC material.
- **The treatment of `Royal` as controlled rather than absolutely prohibited.** The KAP describes a typical position; the course's name material expressly lists `Royal` as a controlled word requiring consent. The answer also flags the point for actual BVI verification.
- **The additional nominee, director-duty, accounting-record, tax-advice and bank-signatory discussion.** Each point is legally relevant to a CSP reviewing this incomplete questionnaire. It does not contradict the KAP.

There is no material incorrect statement, unsupported authority or internal jurisdictional inconsistency that warrants a substantive deduction.
