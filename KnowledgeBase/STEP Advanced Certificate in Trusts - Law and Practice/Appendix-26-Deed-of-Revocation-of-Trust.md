## Appendix 26: Deed of Revocation of Trust

## DATE:

BY: [Name of settlor] ('the Settlor ').

## RECITALS

- (A) This Deed is supplemental to a Deed of Settlement between the Settlor and [Name of trustee] ('the Trustee ') made on [date] known as the [Name] Trust ('the Trust' ).
- (B) The Trustee is the present trustee of the Trust and it is intended that this Deed will forthwith be served upon it.
- (C) By clause [x] of the Trust, the Settlor has reserved the power to revoke the Trust, in whole or in part.

## OPERATIVE PROVISIONS

## NOW THIS DEED WITNESSES

1. THAT the Settlor hereby revokes the Trust in full.
2. THAT by such revocation, the Trustee shall henceforth hold the Trust Fund for and on behalf of the Settlor absolutely.

## Alternatively

3. THAT the Settlor hereby revokes the Trust in relation to that part of the Trust Fund described in the Schedule ('the Assets of the Trust Fund Subject to Partial Revocation' ), with the intent that the Assets of the Trust Fund Subject to Partial Revocation shall henceforth be held by the Trustee for and on behalf of the Settlor absolutely, and that the remaining assets held in the Trust Fund shall continue to be held by the Trustee subject to the trusts of the Trust.

## EXECUTION

IN WITNESS whereof the Settlor has executed and delivered this Deed of Revocation as a deed on the date stated at the beginning of it.

Signed as a deed and delivered by:

in the presence of:
