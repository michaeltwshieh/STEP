# Appendix 21: Trustees' Resolution to Exercise a Discretionary Power to Terminate a Life Interest

## Appendix 21:

## Trustees' Resolution to Exercise a Discretionary Power to Terminate a Life Interest

## [NAME OF TRUST COMPANY]

## WRITTEN RESOLUTION OF THE DIRECTORS OF [ NAME ] TRUST COMPANY  (' the Trustee') IT IS NOTED THAT

- (A) This Resolution is made in respect of a Deed of Settlement between [Settlor] and the Trustee made on [date] known as the [Name] Trust ('the Trust' ).   The Trustee is the present trustee of the Trust and passes this resolution solely in its capacity as Trustee of such Trust.
- (B) Clause [x] of the Trust confers upon the Trustee a discretionary power to terminate the life interest of Beneficiary A and the Trustee wishes to exercise such power.
- (C) The Trustee [and the Company underlying the Trust that holds the Trust Fund] has rendered trust [and company] accounts for the financial year beginning [date] calculated up to the date hereof, and paid all known expenses and Beneficiary B has approved such accounts by signing the same.
- (D) The Trustee is satisfied that the trust will be brought to an end by the termination of the life interest and that Beneficiary B will be entitled to the Trust Fund absolutely.
- (E) Beneficiary B is prepared to release and discharge the Trustee from claims relating to the administration of the Trust and to indemnify the Trustee in respect of future trust expenses, taxes and other claims that may arise in the future in the manner hereafter appearing.

## IT IS RESOLVED

1. THAT in exercise of the power conferred by clause [x] of the Trust, the Trustees hereby resolve to terminate the life interest of Beneficiary A [and thereby bring forward the interest in remainder of Beneficiary B].
2. THAT a copy of this resolution be served upon Beneficiary A [and that the relevant trust officer of the Trustee be and is hereby authorised to effect such service forthwith].
3. THAT the Trustee shall transfer forthwith the legal title to the various assets that comprise the Trust Fund to Beneficiary B [and that the relevant trust officer be and is hereby authorised to prepare and execute on behalf of the Trustee all necessary documentation to fulfil the intent of this resolution].
4. [THAT the Trustee shall and hereby irrevocably declares that it holds shares of the Company that underlies the Trust for and on behalf of Beneficiary B, freed and discharged from the terms of the Trust].

## SIGNED
