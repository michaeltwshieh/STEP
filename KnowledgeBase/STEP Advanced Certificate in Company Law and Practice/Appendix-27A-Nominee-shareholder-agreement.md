# Appendix 27A: Nominee shareholder agreement

## Appendix 27A: Nominee shareholder agreement

## DATED

## BETWEEN

- (1) [Name and address of registered shareholder] (the 'Nominee Shareholder')
- (2) [Name and address of beneficial owner] (the 'Beneficial Owner')

## REICITALS

- (A) The entire issued share capital (the 'Shares') of AB Limited (the 'Managed Company') have been issued to, and are held in registered form by, the Nominee Shareholder.
- (B) The Nominee shareholders acknowledges hereby that he holds such shares according to the terms and conditions herein stated.

## WHEREBY IT IS AGREED

In consideration of the Beneficial Owner conferring rights and other valuable consideration upon the Nominee Shareholder, the Nominee Shareholder hereby Irrevocably declares and agrees that it shall:

1. Hold the Shares of the Managed Company registered in its name as nominee for the Beneficial Owner.
2. Shall not transfer, deal with or dispose of the Shares save as the Beneficial Owner may from time to time direct.
3. Hereby assign to the Beneficial Owner the right to receive any dividends which may be declared and become payable on the Shares to the Nominee Shareholder together with all profits and other moneys that may be paid or payable from time to time upon the Shares.
4. Shall exercise the voting rights and powers as holder of the Shares in such manner and for such purposes as the Beneficial Owner may from time to time direct or determine.

## DATED

SIGNED
