# Appendix 5: Deed of Removal and Appointment of Trustee ('DORA')

## Appendix 5:

## Deed of Removal and Appointment of Trustee ('DORA')

## DATE

## PARTIES

1. [New Trustee, name and address] (the 'New Trustee'); and
2. [Outgoing Trustee, name and address] (the 'Outgoing Trustee'); and
3. [Name and address] (the 'Settlor').

## RECITALS

- (A) This Deed is supplemental to a Deed of Settlement made by the Settlor and the Outgoing Trustee on [date] known as the [name of the trust] ('the Trust').
- (B) Pursuant to clause [x] of the Trust, the Settlor has the power exercisable by deed of removing a trustee from office and the power of appointing a new trustee in place of any trustee that has been so removed. [Pursuant to clause [x] of the Trust, the Outgoing Trustee may resign by giving 14 days' notice in writing to the Settlor [Protector], or such shorter period as may be agreed in writing between the Retiring Trustee and the Settlor [Protector].
- (C) By clause [  ] of the Trust the power of appointing new trustees is vested in the Settlor.
- (D) In exercise of the powers conferred upon him [her] by clause [x] of the Trust, the Settlor wishes to remove the Outgoing Trustee and to appoint the New Trustee to act as a Trustee of the Trust in place of the Outgoing Trustee. [The Retiring Trustee is the present sole trustee of the Trust and wishes to be discharged from the trusts of the Trust.]
- (E) It is intended that as soon as practicable the assets subject to the trusts of the Trust shall be transferred to, or entity under the control of, the New Trustee.

## NOW THIS DEED WITNESSES as follows:

## 1. Removal of Outgoing Trustee

In exercise of the power conferred by clause [x] of the Trust, the Settlor hereby removes the Outgoing Trustee as trustee of the Trust with immediate effect and the Outgoing Trustee is henceforth discharged from the trusts of the Trust. [The Retiring Trustee hereby retires as Trustee and is hereby discharged from the trusts of the Trust.]

## 2. Appointment of New Trustee

In exercise of the powers conferred by clause [x] of the Trust, the Settlor appoints the New Trustee as trustee of the Trust and the New Trustee hereby accepts the appointment.

## 3. Vesting of the Trust Fund

- 3.1 The parties declare that the property comprised in the Trust Fund shall, upon the execution hereof, vest in the New Trustee.
- 3.2 The Outgoing Trustee hereby promises to execute all documents and take all such other action as is necessary for the vesting of the Trust Fund in the New Trustee, following which the New Trustee shall hold the Trust Fund upon the trusts of the Trust

## 4. Release of Outgoing Trustee

The New Trustee, so far as it is competent to do so, hereby releases the Outgoing Trustee from all claims, demands, proceedings and accounts of any kind held by any person, whether in existence or not, actually or prospectively interested under the Trust to the Trust Fund or its income for any act or thing done or omitted to be done in the execution of the Trust except

- (i) those arising from the fraud, dishonesty or the wilful commission of an act known by the trustee to be a breach of trust, and
- (ii) to recover from the Outgoing Trustee the property that comprises the Trust Fund pursuant to clause 3.2 hereof.

## 5. Indemnity of Outgoing Trustee

- 5.1 The New Trustee, for itself and successors, hereby promises the Outgoing Trustee that the New Trustee will at all times hereafter fully indemnify the Outgoing Trustee against the Liabilities (defined below) PROVIDED THAT
- (i) there shall be excluded from such indemnity Liabilities that the Outgoing Trustee would not have been entitled to reimbursement out of the Trust Fund if the Outgoing Trustee had remained as trustee of the Trust on its present terms
- (ii) the indemnity shall not apply in respect of Liabilities of which the Outgoing Trustee first notifies the New Trustee after the sixth anniversary of the date of this deed;
- (iii) subject to clause 5.2, the extent of the liability of the New Trustee under this indemnity shall be limited to the value of the assets of the Trust Fund in the possession, or under the control, of the New Trustee from time to time
- 5.2 If any part of the Trust Fund is distributed to a Beneficiary, the liability of the New Trustee under the indemnity hereby given shall extend to the value of such distribution unless such recipient Beneficiary agrees to indemnify the Outgoing Trustee by way of a direct promise to the Outgoing Trustee on terms substantially similar to those contained in clause 5.1 hereof or on such terms as may be approved by the Outgoing Trustee, such approval not to be unreasonably withheld.
- 5.3 The New Trustee promises that it will not transfer the Trust Fund to any one or more new trustees of the Trust (a 'Successor Trustee') unless such Successor Trustee shall release and indemnify the Outgoing Trustee in terms that are substantially similar to those contained in clauses 4 and 5 of this deed.

## 6. Definitions

- 6.1 In this deed all terms and expressions shall, where the context permits, have the same defined meanings given to them in the Trust.
- 6.2 'Liabilities' means all actions, proceedings, accounts, claims and demands (and all costs incurred in connection therewith) which may be brought or made by any person in connection with the trusts of the Trust or the Trust Fund from time to time, whether the same shall be enforceable in law or not, including claims and demands made for taxes, duties or fiscal liabilities by a revenue authority of any government in any part of the world in respect of the capital or income of the Trust Fund or in respect of any property transferred by the trustee to any person interested under the Trust.
- 6.3 This deed shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of [jurisdiction of the governing law of the trust].

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the parties have executed this document as their deed and have delivered it on the day and year first above written.
