## WRITTEN RESOLUTION OF THE DIRECTORS OF [NAME] TRUST COMPANY (the ' Trustee' ) IT IS NOTED THAT

- (A) This Resolution is supplemental to a Deed of Settlement between [Settlor] and the Trustee made on [date] known as the [Name] Trust ('the Trust' ).
- (B) The Trustee is the present trustee of the Trust and passes this Resolution solely in its capacity as Trustee of such Trust.
- (C) By clause [x] of the Trust, the Trustee is conferred with a discretionary power of appointment of the income and capital of the Trust Fund in the following terms: [reproduce the clause from the trust instrument]
- (D) [Name of beneficiary] ('the Beneficiary ') is a member of the class of Beneficiaries of the Trust.
- (E) The Trustee wishes to exercise the power of appointment conferred by clause [x] of the Trust by making an absolute appointment to the Beneficiary of the funds specified in the Schedule (the ' Appointed Fund' ) in the manner hereafter set out.
- (F) The Trustee having considered carefully all relevant circumstances relating to the exercise of its discretionary powers conferred by the Trust. 4
- (G) [The Trustee shall hold the Appointed Fund for and on behalf of the Beneficiary and the Beneficiary has agreed to release and indemnify the Trustee in the manner hereafter set out.]
- (H) In this Resolution all definitions, terms and expressions shall, where the context so permits and unless otherwise stated herein, have the meanings respectively ascribed to them by the Trust.

## THE TRUSTEE HEREBY RESOLVES as follows

## 1. Appointment

In exercise of the power of appointment conferred by clause [x] of the Trust, the Trustee hereby irrevocably appoints and declares that the Appointed Fund shall henceforth be held by the Trustee for the Beneficiary absolutely, freed and discharged from the terms of the Trust.

4. Trustees do not have to give reasons for taking a particular decision; Re Londonderry's Settlement [1965] Ch 916. But it is considered good practice to actually record that all relevant considerations have been taken into account when reaching a decision.

## 2. Payment of tax and expenses

Any tax, costs and expenses and other liabilities occasioned by the appointment shall be borne by the Beneficiary.

## 3. Trustee's lien

Nothing in this Resolution shall prejudice or impair in any way any lien or charge to which the Trustee is entitled in respect of any liabilities for which it may be or become accountable.
