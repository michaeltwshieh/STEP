## Appendix 30E:

## Notice of final general meeting of the company

## AB LIMITED (IN MEMBERS' VOLUNTARY WINDING UP) NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN pursuant to [s.\_ Companies

## Legislation] THAT:

The final General Meeting of the Company will take place on [date] at [venue] at [time] for the purposes of having an account laid before the members showing the manner in which the manner of the winding up has been conducted and the property of the Company disposed of, hearing any explanation that may be given by the Liquidators and directing by [special] resolution the manner in which the books, accounts and papers of the Company and the Liquidators shall be disposed.

A member entitled to attend and vote at the meeting is entitled to appoint a proxy to attend and [on a poll] vote instead of him. A proxy need not be a member. The instrument appointing a proxy should be deposited at the registered office of the Company not less than 48 hours before the time fixed for the meeting.
