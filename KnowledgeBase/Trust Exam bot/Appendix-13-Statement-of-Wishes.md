## ACKNOWLEDGEMENT

The contents of this memorandum are not binding upon the Trustees and should not be considered binding insofar as I do not intend to restrict the exercise of the Trustees' discretionary powers in any way. Its purpose is to provide guidance and to provide aims, background and the principles I wish the Trustees to consider in the administration of the Trust.

## GUIDANCE

## 1. Overall aim

[To hold and manage my wealth for the benefit of myself and my immediate family during my lifetime. After my death to consolidate, protect and preserve such wealth for the benefit of my children and grandchildren and ensure it stays within the family to be enjoyed reasonably by future generations.]

## 2. Distributions to Beneficiaries

- [2.1A  During my lifetime please consider making distributions to me (or as directed by me) in order to keep me and my immediate family in the manner to which we are accustomed.]
- [2.2A  After my death, ensure my spouse in reasonably provided for.]
- [2.3A  Subject to that, I would like you to regard my children as the principal beneficiaries.]

## Alternatively

- [2.1B I wish to express my firm desire (without binding you) that you should transfer the sum of US$[amount] to my child [X] upon her marriage and the sum of US$[amount] to my child [Y] upon her reaching the age of 30 years.]
- [2.2B Subject to that I express my desire (without binding you) that you should regard my granddaughter [Z] as the principal beneficiary and consider making annual distributions to her of in the region of US$[amount], to be adjusted upwards in the future for inflation.]
