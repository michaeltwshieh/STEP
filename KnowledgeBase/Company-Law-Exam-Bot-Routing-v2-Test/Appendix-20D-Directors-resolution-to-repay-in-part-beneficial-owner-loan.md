## Appendix 20D: Director's resolution to repay, in part, beneficial owner loan

## AB Limited (Company [number]) WRITTEN RESOLUTIONS OF THE SOLE DIRECTOR

## OF AB LIMITED (the 'Company')

Pursuant to art.[x] of the Company's Articles of Association, the undersigned sole director of the Company for the time being:

## HEREBY NOTES:

- (A) THAT [name of beneficial owner] (the 'Creditor') lent the sum of $[1 million] to the Company on an unsecured basis, repayable on demand without interest, evidenced by a Promissory Note by the Company dated [date] (the 'Promissory Note').
- (B) THAT [name of beneficial owner] has made a written demand for re-payment in part of the indebtedness dated [date] and sought the sum of $[amount] be transferred to his account on [date] (the 'Demand).

## HEREBY RESOLVES:

1. THAT a payment to the Creditor in order to comply with the Demand according to the terms of the Promissory Note be and is hereby approved.
2. THAT the sum of $[amount] be repaid and transferred to the Creditor on [date] by wire transfer to the Creditor's account referred to in the Demand.
3. THAT the Creditor's running account with the Company be updated accordingly to reflect such re-payment.

DATE

SIGNED
