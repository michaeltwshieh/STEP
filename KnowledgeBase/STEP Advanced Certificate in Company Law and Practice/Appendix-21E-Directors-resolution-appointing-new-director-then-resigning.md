# Appendix 21E: Director's resolution appointing new director then resigning

## Appendix 21E:

## Director's resolution appointing new director then resigning

## AB Limited (Company [number]) WRITTEN RESOLUTONS OF THE SOLE DIRECTOR

## OF AB LIMITED (the 'Company')

Pursuant to art.[x] of the Company's Articles of Association, the undersigned sole director of the Company for the time being:

## HEREBY NOTES:

- (A) THAT a letter from [name of outgoing director] dated [date] resigning as director of the Company with effect from [date].
- (B) THAT [name of incoming director] has given his/its written consent to be appointed as a director.

## HEREBY RESOLVES

1. THAT, having his written consent to be appointed, [name incoming director] be appointed as director of the Company with effect from the date of this resolution.
2. THAT by letter addressed to the Company and attached to these resolutions marked 'AB1' the [outgoing sole director] resigns from the board and such resignation be and is hereby accepted.
3. THAT the secretary/registered agent/service provider be and is hereby instructed to notify the registrar of companies and update the register of directors of the Company accordingly.

DATED

SIGNED
