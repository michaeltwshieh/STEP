# Appendix 24B: Director's resolution removing secretary (and variation recording resignation)

## Appendix 24B:

## Director's resolution removing secretary (and variation recording resignation)

## AB LIMITED (Company [number])

## WRITTEN RESOLUTIONS OF THE SOLE DIRECTOR OF AB LIMITED

Pursuant to art.[x] of the Company's Articles of Association, we the undersigned sole director of the Company for the time being:

## RESOLVE

1. THAT [Name] be removed as company secretary.
2. THAT [Name], having consented to act, is hereby appointed as secretary of the Company in his place.

DATED

SIGNED

[Alternatively,

1. THAT the resignation of [name] as secretary of the Company be accepted.
2. THAT [Name], having consented to act, is hereby appointed as secretary of the Company in his place.]
