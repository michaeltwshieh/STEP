## RECITALS

- (A) By clause [x] of the Will of [Name] (the 'Testator') dated [date] (the 'Will') the Testator appointed the Proposed Trustee as trustee of the trusts set out therein (the 'Trust').

- (B) The Testator died on [date] without having revoked his [her] Will.

- (C) The Proposed Trustee (i) having never consented to act as trustee of the Trust, and (b) without performing any act or doing anything that could constitute acceptance of such appointment, wishes to disclaim the appointment as trustee of the Trust.

## OPERATIVE PROVISIONS

The Proposed Trustee hereby disclaims the appointment as trustee of the Trust set out in the Will of the Testator and all the property held in the Trust.
