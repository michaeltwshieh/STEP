# Appendix 30C: Notice of winding up for publication in press and Gazette

## Appendix 30C:

## Notice of winding up for publication in press and Gazette

## AB LIMITED (IN MEMBERS' VOLUNTARY WINDING UP)

NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN pursuant to the provisions of [s.\_ Companies Legislation] THAT:

1. The creditors of the above company, which is being wound up voluntarily, are requested to send their names, addresses and descriptions, together with full particulars of their debts or claims to [names and address of liquidators] who are acting as the liquidators of the company, on or before [date].
2. If so required by written notice from the Liquidators, such creditors shall attend and prove their debts or claims at such time and place as shall be communicated to them by notice.
3. If creditors or claimants do not comply with these requests to send particulars or to attend and to prove their debts, then the Liquidators may exclude such creditors from the benefit of any distribution of the assets of the Company.

DATED

SIGNED
