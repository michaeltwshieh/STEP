# Appendix 23: Trustees' Resolution to Appoint an Investment Manager

## Appendix 23:

## Trustees' Resolution to Appoint an Investment Manager

## [NAME OF TRUST COMPANY] WRITTEN RESOLUTION OF THE DIRECTORS OF [ NAME ] TRUST COMPANY  (' the Trustee' ) IT IS NOTED THAT

- (A) This Resolution is made in respect of a Deed of Settlement between [Settlor] and the Trustee made on [date] known as the [Name] Trust ('the Trust' ).   The Trustee is the present trustee of the Trust and passes this resolution solely in its capacity as Trustee of such Trust.
- (B) Clause [x] of the Trust confers upon the Trustee a power to delegate by engaging agents to transact business on behalf, and in connection with the administration, of the Trust.
- (C) The Trustee requires the services of an investment manager to manage the investment of the Trust Fund.

## IT IS RESOLVED

1. THAT in exercise of the power conferred by clause [x] of the Trust, the Trustee hereby appoints {Name of firm] ('the Investment Manager' ) as investment manager to manage the investment of the Trust Fund on a discretionary basis.
2. THAT the terms and conditions set forth and recorded in a draft Investment Management Agreement between the Trustee and the Investment Manager, a copy of which is attached hereto and forms part of these resolutions ('the Investment Management Agreement'), be and are hereby approved.
3. THAT a director be and is hereby authorised to execute the Investment Management Agreement on behalf of the Trustee. [That the authorised representative of the Trustee be and is hereby authorised to procure the underlying company that holds the Trust Fund for and on behalf of the Trust to approve and enter into the Investment Management Agreement.]
4. THAT the authorised officers of the Trustee [Underlying Company] be and are hereby authorised to do and perform any and such acts, including the transfer of money, securities or assets held in the Trust Fund to the brokerage account of the Investment Manager, as such officers shall deem necessary or advisable to carry out the purposes of the foregoing resolutions.

DATE

SIGNED
