# Appendix 13A: Board resolutions to re-register as a private company

## Appendix 13A:

## Board resolutions to re-register as a private company

## AB Limited (Company [number])

## WRITTEN RESOLUTIONS OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF AB LIMITED (the 'Company')

Pursuant to art.[x] of the Company's Articles of Association, the undersigned directors of the Company for the time being, HEREBY:

1. NOTE THAT the Company is a public company limited by shares, having been incorporated in [jurisdiction] on [date] pursuant to [companies legislation] and has been allocated company number [number].
2. RESOLVE that it is desirable and in the interests of the Company for it to seek re-registration as a private company.
3. RESOLVE that it is appropriate in the circumstances to adopt new Articles in the form attached and marked 'AB1' .
4. RESOLVE that a general meeting of the Company be convened to consider and, if thought fit passing, a special resolution to re-register as a private company and adopt new articles. The secretary/corporate services provider be and is hereby instructed to convene such meeting.
5. RESOLVE that, subject to the passing of the special resolution referred to above, the secretary/registered agent/corporate administrator be and is hereby authorised to complete all necessary and desirable actions and procedures to enable the Company to be re-registered as a private company as aforesaid.

DATE
