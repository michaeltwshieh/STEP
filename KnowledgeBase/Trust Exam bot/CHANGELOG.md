## 2026-08-16 — created

**Content** was generated from the two source PDFs (`Course Manual.pdf`, `Appendices.pdf`) by `convert_pdfs.py` then `split_corpus.py`: 11 modules, 37 appendices, `Content.md`. All 350 footnotes were recovered from the PDF text layer and attached to their modules; content coverage was verified against the source with zero loss.

Module titles came from the **course manual's own Contents page**, not the syllabus — CLT International did not supply a syllabus PDF for this course, which is why there is no `Syllabus.md` here. The in-body `## Module N:` headings could not be used: the PDF wraps the longer ones mid-title.

**Instruction files** (`CLAUDE.md`, `section-a.md`, `section-b.md`) were adapted from the Company Law set in `../Company Law Exam Bot/` — the July 2026 revision, whose own history is in that folder's `CHANGELOG.md`. What changed:

- **Subject framing** rewritten from offshore company law (BVI Business Companies, Table A articles, the Companies Acts) to offshore trusts: the express trust and its instrument, offshore trust legislation, reserved powers and firewall provisions, protectors, purpose trusts, private foundations. The guard rule was kept and re-pointed at onshore English trust law.
- **Module count** 12 → 11, and every cross-module routing example replaced with real Trusts pairs (protector's removal power at Module 7 §2.2.1 against the instrument's provision at Module 5 §4, and so on).
- **The `Syllabus.md` routing aid was dropped**, since no such file exists for this exam.
- **Both worked examples in `section-b.md` rebuilt.** The coverage block now runs on a discretionary-trust advance grounded in Module 4 §1.5–1.7; the drafting block on `Appendix 8` (Trustee's Resolution: to Distribute Income), with its checklist matched to that precedent's actual structure.
- **~20 terminology swaps** through the style rules — member/director/liquidator/articles became beneficiary/trustee/protector/trust instrument, and the letter-precedent pointer moved from Company Law's Appendix 21F to Appendices 14B and 13.
- **Citation examples** changed to authorities verified present in this manual: *Saunders v Vautier*, *Ilott v Mitson [2017] UKSC 17*, *section 2 of the International Trusts Act 1984*.

Carried over **unchanged**, being exam-neutral: the burstiness, perplexity and structural-asymmetry rules, the banned-word clusters, the em-dash ban, and the coverage and question-risk machinery.

**Verified after adaptation:** every `Module N` and `Appendix <label>` reference resolves against this folder's own `Content.md`; no module above 11; residual Company Law term sweep clean.
