# Appendix 24A: Director's resolution appointing a secretary

## Appendix 24A:

## Director's resolution appointing a secretary

## AB LIMITED (Company [number])

## WRITTEN RESOLUTIONS OF THE SOLE DIRECTOR OF AB LIMITED

Pursuant to art.[x] of the Company's Articles of Association, we the undersigned sole director of the Company for the time being:

## NOTE :

- (A) THAT [name] has consented in writing to be appointed as company secretary.
- (B) THAT in order to ensure the conduct and administration of the Company complies with the requirements of applicable company law, the board wishes to delegate the responsibilities set out in the attached role and description document [Appendix 24C] upon the secretary (the 'Role Description').

## RESOLVE :

1. THAT [Name], having consented to act, is hereby appointed as secretary of the Company.
2. THAT the Role Description be and is hereby approved and the responsibilities set out therein be assigned and delegated to the secretary.

DATED

SIGNED
