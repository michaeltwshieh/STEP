# Appendix 29B: General power of attorney in favour of beneficial owner

## Appendix 29B:

## General power of attorney in favour of beneficial owner

## DATED

## BETWEEN

[Name and address of managed company] (the 'Company'), and

[Name and address of beneficial owner] (the 'Attorney')

## OPERATIVE PROVISIONS

1. The Company hereby appoints the Attorney to be its attorney.
2. The Attorney may exercise and execute in the name, and on behalf, of the Company all powers and discretions conferred upon its board to generally manage the affairs and business of the Company, including but not limited to the power to negotiate, make offers and bid on behalf of the Company, enter into, and execute in the name of the Company all contracts deeds and other documents.

IN WITNESS the parties have executed and delivered this Power of Attorney as a deed.
