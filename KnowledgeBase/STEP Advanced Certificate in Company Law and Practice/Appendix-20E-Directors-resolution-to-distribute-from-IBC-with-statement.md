# Appendix 20E: Director's resolution to distribute from IBC, with statement of solvency

## Appendix 20E:

## Director's resolution to distribute from IBC, with statement of solvency

## AB Limited (Company [number])

## WRITTEN RESOLUTIONS OF THE SOLE DIRECTOR OF AB LIMITED (the 'Company')

Pursuant to art.[x] of the Company's Articles of Association, the undersigned sole director of the Company for the time being:

## HEREBY NOTES:

- (A) THAT the director has carried out a careful review of the Company's financial statements and established that there are distributable funds held in the Company that justify a distribution.

## HEREBY DECLARES:

- (B) THAT the director is satisfied that the Company will, immediately after the proposed distribution [and for the 12 months thereafter], 29 satisfy the statutory solvency test by:
- (i) having assets that exceed its liabilities, and
- (ii) being able to pay its debts as they fall due for payment.

## HEREBY RESOLVES

1. THAT a distribution [dividend] in respect of the year ended [date] be paid [declared], payable forthwith at the rate of [amount] per ordinary share to shareholders registered at the close of business on [date].
2. THAT the corporate services provider be and is hereby instructed to wire transfer the sum of $[amount] from the Company's account at [bank] to the account of [account] as in compliance with the sole shareholder's distribution [dividend payment] mandate.

DATE

SIGNED

29. Some jurisdiction require the solvency test to be satisfied at a point in time immediately after the proposed distribution; other jurisdiction require the directors to certify that the company will remain solvent for a stated period thereafter, such as 12 months. When drafting the resolution care should be taken to ensure the correct solvency test applicable in the jurisdiction of incorporation is reproduced.
